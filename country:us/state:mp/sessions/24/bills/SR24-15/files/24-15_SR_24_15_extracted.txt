TWENTY-FOURTH NORTHERN MARIANAS COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE
THIRD REGULAR SESSION, 2026 S.R. NO. 24-15
A SENATE RESOLUTION
Recognizing and honoring the Life, Service, and Legacy of the
Honorable Ana Atalig Sablan Teregeyo for her distinguished
service and significant contributions to the Commonwealth of the
Northern Mariana Islands and expressing the profound sympathy
and condolences of the Senate on the Occasion of Her Passing.
1 WHEREAS, the members of this Legislative Body are pleased to honor residents
2 of the Commonwealth for their remarkable service and sacrifices for the betterment of the
3 livelihoods, liberties, and freedoms of the citizens of the Commonwealth of the Northern
4 Mariana Islands and the entire United States of America; and
5 WHEREAS, the Honorable Ana Atalig Sablan Teregeyo was born on February 9,
6 1948 to parents, †Tun Benigno Olupomar Sablan and †Tan Consolacion Hocog Atalig
7 Sablan. Her siblings include, †Vicente A. Sablan, †Micaela S. Demapan, †Herman A.
8 Sablan, †Gregorio M. Sablan, †Juan A. Sablan, †Isidro A. Sablan and †Manuel A.
9 Sablan; and
10 WHEREAS, the Honorable Ana Atalig Sablan Teregeyo was married to Señot
11 Joaquin Teregeyo and they shared six children, †Irene, Geraldine, Donald, Dennis,
12 Catherine, and Derek; and
13 WHEREAS, the Honorable Ana Atalig Sablan Teregeyo attended Marianas High
14 School (1971) and the University of Nebraska in Lincoln (1972-1976); and
15 WHEREAS, that Honorable Ana Atalig Sablan Teregeyo began her government
16 career early working for the Trust Territory Government on Saipan in such positions as
17 Management Intern for the Personnel Department, Employment Development and
18 Training Specialist and Program Administrator for CETA from 1975-1980; and
19 WHEREAS, the Honorable Ana Atalig Sablan Teregeyo was subsequently
20 appointed as the first Executive Director of the CNMI Council for Arts and Culture, a
21 position she held from 1980 to 1991. As executive director, the Honorable Ana Atalig

18 inspired many to pursue public service; and
19 WHEREAS, her passing on February 27, 2026 is a profound loss to the
20 Commonwealth, and her legacy of dedication and public service will continue to resonate

22 contributions and expresses its heartfelt condolences to her family and loved ones during
23 this time of mourning;
