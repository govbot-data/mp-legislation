LEGISLATURE
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
SESSION, 2026p H. L. B. 24- 4(1
A LOCAL APPROPRIATION BILL FOR AN ACT
FOR THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT
To appropriate One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000.00) for the
continued operation and regulatory oversight of the Rota Casino
Gaming Commission; and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE IRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT
DELEGATION PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF
THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. PURPOSE.
2 The Rota Local Law 24-05 under Section 2(c) reserved $200,000 for
3 appropriation by the Rota Legislative Delegation. See Rota Local Law 24-05,
4 Section 2(c).
5 The purpose of this bill is to appropriate the local funds for One
6 HundredThousand Dollars ($100,000.00) for the continued operation and
7 regulatory oversight of the Rota Casino Gaming Commission and other needs of
8 the municipality.
9 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.

HOUSE LOCAL BILL 24- it 97
1 Notwithstanding any provisions, statutes, laws, or regulations to the
2 contrary, the Rota Legislative Delegation hereby appropriates, from the reserved
3 funds under Rota Local Law 24-05, Section 2(c), One Hundred Thousand Dollars
4 ($100,000.00) to the Rota Casino Gaming Commission (RCGC) for its operations
5 and other needs of the municipality to be used as follows:
6 (a) $50,000 for the RCGC Operations; and
7 (b) $50,000 for the payment of salaries of the previous RCGC members.
8 SECTION 3. EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY.
9 The RCGC Chairperson shall be the expenditure authority of the funds
10 under Section 2. Any compensation owed to a former Commission Member shall
11 be paid in accordance with 4 CMC § 9711.
12 SECTION 4. REPROGRAMMING.
13 Notwithstanding any laws, provisions, or regulations to the contrary, funds
14 appropriated under this Act shall not be reprogrammed for any other purpose unless
15 a written justification is submitted to the RLD Chairperson for approval. Three
16 RLD members shall sign the approval of the requested reprogramming.
17 SECTION 5. REPORTING.
18 The expenditure authority shall submit detailed monthly reports showing
19 the expenditure of funds appropriated under this Act. These reports shall be
20 furnished to the Chairpersons of the Rota Municipal Council and the First
21 Senatorial Delegation until all appropriated funds have been fully expended. If the
