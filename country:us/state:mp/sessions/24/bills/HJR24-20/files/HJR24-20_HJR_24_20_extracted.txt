A HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION
To approve the Governor's request to exempt and fill a critical and
vacant personnel position within the Executive Branch, specifically
for the Bureau of Environmental and Coastal Quality (BECQ),
pursuant to Public Law 24-20, Section 602(a).
1 WHEREAS, the Twenty-Fourth Northern Marianas Commonwealth
2 Legislature enacted Public Law 24-20, also known as the Revised Appropriations
3 and Budget Authority Act of 2026; and
4 WHEREAS, Section 602(a) of Public Law 24-20 mandates a freeze on
5 filling new or vacant positions within the Executive Branch, except for positions
6 essential to the delivery of public services; and
7 WHEREAS, Section 602(a) provides a mechanism to exempt critical
8 vacancies from this freeze upon the formal determination and certification of the
9 Governor, subject to approval by the Legislature via a joint resolution; and
10 WHEREAS, the Bureau of Environmental and Coastal Quality (BECQ) is
11 responsible to implement and enforce the Commonwealth Litter Control Act of
12 1989, as amended, codified at 2 CMC §§ 3411-3416, and to ensure that the
13 responsibilities assigned to the Division of Environmental Quality (DEQ) under the

1 Act continue to be carried out effectively; The Litter Control Program is an
2 important component of the Commonwealth's environmental protection and public
3 health responsibilities; DEQ is responsible for enforcement of the Litter Control
4 Act, public education and outreach, and the training and certification of Litter
5 Control Apprehending Officers from participating government agencies; and
6 WHEREAS, the need for this position has become increasingly important
7 due to the continuing challenges associated with illegal dumping, littering, post-
8 disaster debris, construction waste, and the improper disposal of solid waste
9 throughout the Commonwealth; Effective enforcement requires dedicated
10 personnel who can investigate complaints, conduct field inspections and patrols,
11 document violations, identify responsible parties, coordinate with other
12 enforcement agencies, issue or support citations and enforcement actions, and
13 prepare cases for further administrative or legal action; and
14 WHEREAS, the requested position is also necessary to support the
15 coordinated enforcement responsibilities of the Commonwealth's various agencies
16 participating in the Litter Control Program; Without adequate DEQ personnel
17 dedicated to this function, enforcement becomes largely complaint-driven and the
18 Commonwealth's ability to investigate violations and hold violators accountable is
19 significantly reduced; and
20 WHEREAS, leaving the position of Litter Control Officer vacant directly
21 compromises the Commonwealth's ability to enforce anti-littering laws, address
