A HOUSE RESOLUTION
To congratulate, honor, and pay tribute to the Saipan Chamber of
Commerce on the occasion of its Golden Jubilee, marking fifty (50)
years since its incorporation in 1976 and more than six and a half
decades of service to the business community and people of the
Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands since its founding
in 1959.
1 WHEREAS, in late 1959, when Saipan's population was still small, with
2 few roads, and an economy still emerging from the aftermath of the Second World
3 War, a group of visionary entrepreneurs came together in lateral Quonset huts at
4 the former Saipan and Royal Taga Hotels to form a loose association of businesses
5 dedicated to advancing commerce, opportunity, and prosperity for the people of
6 these islands; and
7 WHEREAS, the late Jose C. "Joeten" Tenorio served as the first President
8 of the Saipan Chamber of Commerce and, in that capacity, represented the interests
9 of the local business community during the political negotiation and transition
10 period from Trust Territory administration to Commonwealth status with the
11 United States of America; and
