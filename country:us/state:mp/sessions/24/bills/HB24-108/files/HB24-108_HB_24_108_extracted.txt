13 Section 403. Donation of Sick Leave.
14 Notwithstanding any laws to the contrary, when an employee volunteers to
15 donate sick leave, the donated sick leave shall be based on the donor's current base
16 hourly salary and shall be converted into equivalent sick leave hours based on the
17 recipient's current base hourly salary. Provided that the donated sick leave hours
18 shall not exceed 1040 hours.'
1 Conversion:
(Sick Leave Hours Donated) multiplied by (Donor's Hourly Base Rate) = (Donor's Donated
Dollar Value)
(Donor's Donated Dollar Value) divided by (Recipient's Hourly Base Rate) = (Number of Hours
Donated to Recipient)
Example: (Donor donated 100 hours) X (Donor's hourly rate of $20) = $2,000
($2,000) divided by (Recipient's hourly rate of $10) = 200 hours to Recipient
