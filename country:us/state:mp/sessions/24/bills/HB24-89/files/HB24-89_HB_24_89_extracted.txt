A BILL FOR AN ACT
To appropriate $300,000.00 of the dividends collected by the
Commonwealth Economic Development Authority (CEDA) from
the Commonwealth Utilities Corporation (CUC) to fund
infrastructure projects in the Second Senatorial District.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE 24TH NORTHERN MARIANAS
COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 Section 1. The Legislature finds that the Second Senatorial District requires
2 additional funding to improve and maintain its public infrastructure.
3 Therefore, the purpose of this Act is to appropriate dividends collected by
4 the Commonwealth Economic Development Authority (CEDA) from the
5 Commonwealth Utilities Corporation (CUC) to fund infrastructure projects within
6 the Second Senatorial District.
7 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.
8 Notwithstanding any provision(s) of law, statutes, rules or regulations to the
9 contrary and pursuant to 4 CMC § 10508 to appropriate dividends collected by
10 CEDA from CUC, the Legislature hereby appropriates $300,000.00 of the

3 SECTION 3. REPROGRAMMING.
4 Funds appropriated under this act may be reprogrammed for any other
5 purpose, and notwithstanding any provision of law, all funds appropriated under
6 this Act shall be without fiscal year limitation.
7 SECTION 4. EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY.
8 The expenditure authority of the funds allocated under Section 2 of this Act
9 shall be the Mayor of Tinian. Furthermore, the Mayor of Tinian shall be
10 responsible for reporting all expenses to the Commonwealth Economic
11 Development Authority upon the exhaustion of the funds appropriated under this
12 Act.
