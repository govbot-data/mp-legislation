BE IT ENACTED BY THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT DELEGATION PURSUANT TO
CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. PURPOSE.
2 The purpose of this Act is to re-appropriate Sixty thousand dollars ($60,000.00) allotted
3 under Section 2 (a) of Rota Local Law 24-07 and Twenty thousand dollars ($20,000.00) allotted
4 under Rota Local Law 24-08 collected from poker fees in the First Senatorial District to respond
5 to critical emergency situations and urgent recovery initiatives in the aftermath of Super Typhoon
6 Sinlaku.
7 SECTION 2. RE-APPROPRIATION.
8 Notwithstanding any provision(s) of law, statutes, rules or regulations to the contrary and
9 pursuant to funds allocated to the First Senatorial District from poker fees, the Rota Legislative
