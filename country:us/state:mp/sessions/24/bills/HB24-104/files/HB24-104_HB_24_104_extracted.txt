A BILL FOR AN ACT
To earmark the Phyto Sanitary Certificate and the
Certificate of Origin fees collected by the Customs
and Bio-Security, and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE 24TH NORTHERN MARIANAS
COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 Section 1. Findings and Purpose.
2 The Legislature finds that currently, the Office of Customs and Bio-Security
3 charges $10.00 for a Phytosanitary Certificate and $5.00 fora Certificate of Origin.
4 The fees collected are deposited in the CNMI Treasury.
5 The Legislature finds that the Office of Customs and Bio-Security in each
6 senatorial district needs additional funds for the respective offices.
7 Therefore, the purpose of this Act is to earmark the fees collected so that the
8 fees remain in the senatorial district in which the fees are collected.
9 Section 2. Enactment. Subject to the codification by the Commonwealth
10 Law Revision, the following is enacted to read:
