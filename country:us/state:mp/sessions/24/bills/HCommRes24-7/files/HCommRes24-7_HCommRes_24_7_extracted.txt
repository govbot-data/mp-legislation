A HOUSE COMMEMORATIVE RESOLUTION
To recognize and commend the Pioneer Contestants and
Titleholders of the Miss Northern Marianas Descent Beauty Pageant
for their dedication to the preservation, promotion, and advancement
of the Chamorro and Carolinian culture, heritage, and history in the
Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.
1 WHEREAS, the Legislature is proud to recognize those in the community
2 who confidently and creatively uplift both Chamorro and Carolinian cultural
3 identity and women's empowerment on behalf of the Commonwealth of the
4 Northern Mariana Islands; and
5 WHEREAS, the Miss Northern Marianas Descent Beauty Pageant was
established in the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands dedicated to
7 cultural education, identity, and heritage preservation; and
8 WHEREAS, the inaugural pageant was conducted in January 2026,
9 marking a historic milestone in the cultural movement of the Commonwealth; and
10 WHEREAS, the Pioneer Contestants of this inaugural pageant committed
11 themselves to an immersive experience in Chamorro and Carolinian culture,
