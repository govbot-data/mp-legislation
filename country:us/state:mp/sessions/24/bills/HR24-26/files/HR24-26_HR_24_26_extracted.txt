A HOUSE RESOLUTION
To recognize Aria Keilbach for her esteemed accomplishments as
Miss Northern Marianas Earth 2025 representing the
Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands in Miss Earth.
1 WHEREAS, the Legislature is proud to recognize those in the community
2 who represent the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands on the
3 international stage; and
4 WHEREAS, Aria Keilbach is a beaming example of an individual who has
5 demonstrated the optimistic and compassionate spirit of those who champion
6 environmental protection and awareness to achieve the Miss Northern Marianas
7 Earth title; and
8 WHEREAS, Aria Keilbach was born in Colorado but moved to Saipan in
9 the Northern Mariana Islands at a very young age. She attended Kagman High
10 School before transferring to Marianas High School where she graduated with
11 honors in 2020. She later enrolled at Northern Marianas College; and
12 WHEREAS, in 2022, Ms. Keilbach founded a nonprofit animal rescue

1 organization and established herself as an animal advocate and social media
2 influencer. She has collaborated with internationally recognized animal welfare
3 organizations. An avid traveler, she has visited Japan, South Korea, the Philippines,
4 London, and Ireland; and
5 WHEREAS, Aria's passions include dog training, reading (especially
6 dystopian and mythical fiction), drawing, and sketching. Her favorite films span
7 true crime documentaries, Disney classics, and mystery thrillers. She admires
8 actress Zendaya and rugby star Ilona Maher; and
9 WHEREAS, Aria's background, interests, and experiences eventually
10 propelled her to enter into Miss Northern Marianas Earth 2025; and
11 WHEREAS, on July 19, 2025, Ms. Keilbach was crowned Miss Northern
12 Marianas Earth 2025 in Saipan. This national title qualified her to represent the
13 Northern Mariana Islands at the prestigious Miss Earth international pageant — one
14 of the world's major beauty pageants alongside Miss Universe, Miss World, and
15 Miss International; and
16 WHEREAS, founded in 2001 by Carousel Productions in the Philippines,
17 Miss Earth is unique among global pageants for its strong environmental focus. The
18 competition channels the influence of beauty queens to promote environmental
19 awareness and protection under the banner "Beauties for a Cause." Each year,
