A LOCAL APPROPRIATION BILL FOR AN ACT
FOR THE THIRD SENATORIAL DISTRICT
To re-appropriate funds under Section 2(t)(d) of Saipan Local Law 23-04 (Project
Code 7190M); and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE THIRD SENATORIAL DISTRICT DELEGATION
PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF THE
COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. PURPOSE.
2 The purpose of this Act is to re-appropriate $60,000.00 from funds under
3 Section 2(t)(d) of Saipan Local Law 23-04 (Project Code 7190M).
4 SECTION 2. RE-APPROPRIATION.
5 Notwithstanding any provision(s) of law, statutes, rules or regulations to the
6 contrary and pursuant to funds allocated to the Third Senatorial District the Saipan
7 and Northern Islands Legislative Delegation (SNILD) hereby re-appropriates
8 $60,000.00 from funds under Section 2(t)(d) of Saipan Local Law 23-04 (Project
9 Code 7190M) as follows:
10 a) Thirty-five Thousand dollars ($35,000.00) to the Natibu Sports
11 Association (NSA) for the repair and improvement of the Capitol Hill
