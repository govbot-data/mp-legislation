A HOUSE COMMEMORATIVE RESOLUTION
To commemorate the life, legacy, and dedicated service of the late
Dr. John Lamphear Doyle III, for his exceptional contributions to
medicine and his profound impact on the well-being of the people
of the CNMI, and to extend sincere condolences to his bereaved
family.
1 WHEREAS, it is incumbent upon the people of the Commonwealth of the
2 Northern Mariana Islands to commend and recognize those esteemed individuals
3 within the medical community for their extraordinary and sustained contributions
4 to the progress of medicine and the improvement of life; and
5 WHEREAS, it is with profound sorrow that the House of Representatives
6 acknowledges the unexpected passing of the late Dr. John Lamphear Doyle III,
7 affectionately known as "Johnny" and "Doc," on May 31, 2025, a man whose life,
8 though cut short, was a testament to service, intellect, and unwavering compassion;
9 and
10 WHEREAS, 'Dr. Doyle, born on August 13, 1958, in Lexington,
11 Kentucky, leaves behind a loving wife, Beatrix Susan Saavedra Doyle, nine
12 devoted children: John "Jack," Gabriel, twins Sean and Rheannon, Alexandria,
