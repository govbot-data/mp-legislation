A BILL FOR AN ACT
To amend the punishment for the crime of vandalism to include

BE IT ENACTED BY THE 24T NORTHERN MARIANAS
COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 Section 1. Findings and Purpose. The Legislature finds that incidents of
2 vandalism continue to occur throughout the Commonwealth of the Northern
3 Mariana Islands, resulting in damage to both public and private property. Between
4 February 2024 and January 2026, there were at least nine reported incidents of
5 vandalism documented by law enforcement, including but not limited to the cutting
6 of residential surveillance camera lines, damage to solar-powered lighting at beach
7 pavilions, vandalism of newly constructed playgrounds, automated teller machines,
8 residential murals, and the deliberate cutting of electrical power lines at public park
9 facilities.
10 The Legislature further finds that, through Public Law 21-18, the offense of
11 vandalism was established to address the proliferation of crimes involving the
12 destruction of public and private property. Prior to the enactment of Public Law 21-

1 18, such conduct was prosecuted as criminal mischief, an offense that included
2 confinement as potential form of punishment. However, the new law left out
3 punishment ranges for confinement.
4 The Legislature finds that the absence of confinement as a sentencing option
5 limits the courts' ability to impose appropriate penalties in cases where the nature
6 of the offense, the extent of the damage, or a defendant's noncompliance with
7 probation conditions would otherwise warrant confinement.
8 The purpose of the Act is to restore confinement as an optional form of
9 punishment for the offense of vandalism. This Act establishes graduated
10 confinement options based on the value of the pecuniary loss resulting from the
11 vandalism, while preserving judicial discretion to impose a sentence that is
12 proportional, appropriate, and consistent with the interests of justice.
13 Section 2. Amendment. 6 CMC §1805 of the Commonwealth Code is
14 hereby amended to read as follows:
15 "§ 1805. Vandalism
16 (a) It shall be unlawful for any person and/or persons to knowingly
17 or intentionally vandalize any public or private property.
18 (b) Any person and/or persons in violation of subsection (a) shall be
19 punished as follows:
20 (1) If the pecuniary loss from the property vandalized is valued at
21 $20,000 or more, by imprisonment for not more than 10 years.

13 (2) Second Offense: A fine of not less than $1,000 but not more than
14 $5,000 and not more than 80 hours of community service.
15 (3) Third and any subsequent offense: A fine of not less than $5,000
16 but not more than $10,000 and not more than 120 hours of community
17 service-after-time-served."
