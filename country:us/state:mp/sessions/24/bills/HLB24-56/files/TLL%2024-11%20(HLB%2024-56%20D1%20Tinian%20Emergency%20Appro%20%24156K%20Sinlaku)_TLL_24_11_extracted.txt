reference.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE SECOND SENATORIAL DISTRICT DELEGATION PURSUANT
TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. PURPOSE.
2 The purpose of this Act is to appropriate One Hundred Fifty-Six Thousand dollars
3 ($156,000.00) from revenues collected from poker fees in the Second Senatorial District to
4 respond to critical emergency situations and urgent recovery initiatives in the aftermath of Super
5 Typhoon Sinlaku. The certification of funds was prepared on March 13, 2026 and April 10, 2026
6 by the Acting Resident Department Head of the Department of Finance, Tinian and is attached for
7 reference. This appropriation of local funds is not part of the FY 2026 Budget Act. Therefore, this
8 local appropriation of local funds does not contravene 1 CMC §7401 and 1 CMC §7404(d) or any

1 other provision of the CNMI Budget Act.
2 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATIONS.
3 Notwithstanding any provision(s) of law, statutes, rules or regulations to the contrary and
4 pursuant to funds allocated to the Second Senatorial District from poker fees, the Tinian and
5 Aguiguan Legislative Delegation hereby appropriates One Hundred Fifty-Six Thousand dollars
6 ($156,000.00) of the revenues collected for the Second Senatorial District from the poker fees as
7 follows:
8 (A) $132,500.00 shall be allocated to the Office of the Mayor of Tinian and Aguiguan.
9 Funds will be allocated for operations, personnel, and public programs to respond to critical
10 emergency situations and urgent recovery initiatives in the aftermath of Super Typhoon Sinlaku.
11 (B) $16,000.00 to the Tinian Casino Gaming Control Commission of which $10,000 shall
12 be used for personnel and operations and $6,000 shall be used to pay the commissioners'
13 compensation.
14 (C) $7,500.00 to the Tinian High School top students of which $5,000 shall be paid to the
15 Valedictorian and $2,500.00 shall be paid to the Salutatorian.
16 SECTION 3. EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY.
17 (a) The expenditure authority for funds under Section 2(A), (B), and (C) shall be the Mayor
18 of Tinian and Aguiguan.

1 SECTION 4. REPROGRAMMING.
2 Funds appropriated under this act shall not be reprogrammed for any other purpose and
3 notwithstanding any provision of law, all funds appropriated under this Act shall be without fiscal
4 year limitation(s).
5 SECTION 5. MANAGEMENT OF FUNDS.
6 The Secretary of Finance is hereby authorized and directed to transfer the sum of One
7 Hundred Fifty-Six Thousand dollars ($156,000.00), or so much thereof as may be necessary, to
8 the Tinian Municipal Treasurer for the local public purposes specified in this Act. This transfer
9 shall not be construed to limit, diminish, or otherwise impair the constitutional or statutory
10 authority of the Secretary of Finance to control, regulate, supervise, audit, and account for the
11 expenditure of public funds. All funds transferred shall remain subject to Commonwealth
12 accounting and audit requirements. The Tinian Municipal Treasurer shall submit monthly
13 expenditure reports, with supporting documentation, to the Secretary of Finance in a form
14 prescribed by the Department of Finance.
15 SECTION 6. REPORTING OF EXPENDITURES.
16 The recipient of the funds allocated under Section 2 of this Act shall be responsible to
17 report all expenses responsible to report all expenses to the Secretary of the Department of Finance
18 by the end of the 2026 calendar year. Provided further that failure to provide all expenses, entities
19 that were afforded funds under this Act shall not be able to avail to any future funds appropriated
20 by the Tinian & Aguiguan Legislative Delegation.
