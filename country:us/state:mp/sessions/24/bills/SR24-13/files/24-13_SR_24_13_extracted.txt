TWENTY-FOURTH NORTHERN MARIANAS COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE
Third Regular Session, 2026 Senate Resolution No. 24-13
A SENATE RESOLUTION
To recognize and commend Honorable Justice Perry
Borja Inos for his long-lasting contributions to the
Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands and
its people.
1 WHEREAS, the members of the Senate of the Twenty-Fourth Northern Marianas
2 Commonwealth Legislature are pleased to pay tribute and honor to the Commonwealth
3 citizens who serve in the capacities as bench members of the Northern Mariana Islands
4 Judiciary and devote their time and efforts to the protection of the Commonwealth people
5 and their livelihoods; and
6 WHEREAS, Honorable Justice Perry Borja Inos was born on the pristine island of
7 Saipan, and has established a life indulged in fond memories with his family, including his
8 wife, Debra A. Inos, and their three children, whose encouragement and support have been
9 integral to his years of dedicated public service; and
10 WHEREAS, Honorable Justice Perry Borja Inos is a proud product of the
11 Commonwealth’s educational institutions, having attended Rota High School. He then
12 persevered into higher education and obtained a Bachelor of Business Administration in
13 Management Degree from the University of Guam, before obtaining his Juris Doctor
14 Degree from the University of New Mexico School of Law; and
15 WHEREAS, Honorable Justice Perry Borja Inos’ career in public service to the
16 Commonwealth is long-standing and has served the community well. Prior to joining the
17 bench, Justice Inos served the Commonwealth with distinction in private and public legal
18 practice, including service with the Office of the Attorney General, the Law Office of John
19 A. Manglona, and as a solo practitioner; and
20 WHEREAS, Honorable Justice Perry Borja Inos has devoted more than seventeen
21 (17) years of exemplary service to the Northern Mariana Islands Judiciary, beginning his

1 judicial career as an Associate Judge of the Superior Court in September 2008 and later
2 ascending to the Supreme Court as an Associate Justice in March 2013. Justice Inos was
3 appointed to both the Superior Court and the Supreme Court by former Governor Benigno
4 R. Fitial and subsequently earned the confidence of the people of the Commonwealth by
5 successfully undergoing judicial retention elections in 2012 and 2020; and
6 WHEREAS, throughout his tenure on the bench, Honorable Justice Perry Borja
7 Inos made substantial and lasting contributions to the development of the law and the
8 administration of justice, serving on panels addressing significant and complex legal issues
9 and consistently upholding the highest standards of judicial integrity, fairness, and
10 professionalism. Justice Inos also served as a voting member of the Judicial Council and
11 provided outstanding leadership as Chair of several Supreme Court Rules Committees and
12 the Judiciary’s Professional Development Committee, helping to shape judicial policy,
13 strengthen institutional practices, and elevate professional standards within the Judiciary;
14 and
15 WHEREAS, Honorable Justice Perry Borja Inos played a pivotal role in judicial
16 education and regional collaboration as a member of the Pacific Judicial Council’s
17 Education Committee, and further assisted the Guam Supreme Court as a Justice Pro Tem
18 when designated; and
19 WHEREAS, Honorable Justice Perry Borja Inos spearheaded the drafting and
20 development of numerous key Judiciary rules and policies, most notably providing
21 leadership on the Interpreter and Translator Code of Conduct Committee, which ensured
22 meaningful access to the courts for limited English proficient individuals and resulted in
23 the adoption of the Judiciary’s Language Access Policy and Plan. Justice Inos was also
24 instrumental in the drafting of the Judiciary’s Procurement Rules, promoting
25 accountability, transparency, and efficiency in government expenditures, and chaired or
26 contributed to other critical committees such as Indigent Representation, Rules Governing
27 Procedure for Traffic and Other Infractions, Rules of Criminal Procedure, Rules of
28 Evidence, and Judiciary Vehicle Policy; and

1 WHEREAS, Honorable Justice Perry Borja Inos played a critical role in
2 advocating for the passage of the Commonwealth Recorder’s Act of 2020 and in drafting
3 the Rules of Electronic Recordation, which together modernized the CNMI court system
4 by enabling the transition from manual, in-person recordation to a comprehensive system
5 of electronic recordation; and
6 WHEREAS, beyond his judicial duties, Honorable Justice Perry Borja Inos
7 demonstrated a deep commitment to public service and community engagement through
8 his participation in Judiciary outreach programs and his volunteer service as a member of
9 the Rotary Club of Saipan;
10 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Senate of the Twenty-Fourth
11 Northern Marianas Commonwealth Legislature hereby joins the people of the
12 Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands in honoring the Honorable Justice Perry
13 Borja Inos for his unyielding years of public service to the people of the Commonwealth
14 of the Northern Mariana Islands; and
15 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the President of the Senate shall certify and
16 the Senate Legislative Secretary shall attest to the adoption of this Resolution, and
17 thereafter the Senate Clerk shall transmit certified copies to Honorable Justice Perry Borja
18 Inos; the Honorable David M. Apatang, Governor of the CNMI; the Honorable Edmund S.
19 Villagomez, Speaker of the House of Representatives; and the Honorable Kimberlyn King-
20 Hinds, CNMI Delegate to the House of Representatives Washington, 119th United States
21 Congress.
