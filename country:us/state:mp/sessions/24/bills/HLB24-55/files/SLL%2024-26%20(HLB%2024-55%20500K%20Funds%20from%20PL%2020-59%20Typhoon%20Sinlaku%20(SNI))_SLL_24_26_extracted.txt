BE IT ENACTED BY THE THIRD SENATORIAL DISTRICT DELEGATION PURSUANT TO
CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Delegation finds that Typhoon Sinlaku caused widespread damage across the
3 Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands (CNMI), resulting in significant disruption to
4 infrastructure, public services, housing and economic activity. The Delegation finds that Typhoon
5 Sinlaku placed significant strain on residents, including but not limited to displacement of families
6 and overcrowding in temporary shelters, increased health risks due to sanitation challenges and
7 limited access to medical care, loss of income for workers and small business owners and
8 psychological stress and trauma associated with the disaster. Given the magnitude of destruction
9 and the limited fiscal capacity of the CNMI external funding assistance is necessary to ensure and
10 equitable and timely recovery. The Delegation finds that without such support, recovery efforts

1 will be significantly delayed, prolonging hardship for residents and increasing long-term economic
2 losses.
3 The Delegation further finds that Typhoon Sinlaku represents a major disaster with far-
4 reaching consequences. The Delegation finds that immediate and sustained funding assistance is
5 critical to restore essential services and rebuild communities. Furthermore, the Delegation finds
6 that strategic investment at this stage will not accelerate recovery but the funding assistance will
7 reduce vulnerability, protect lives and livelihoods in the immediate future.
8 Therefore, the purpose of this Act is to appropriate local funds from the revenues collected
9 for the Third Senatorial District pursuant to Public Law 20-59.
10 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.
11 Notwithstanding any provision(s) of law, statutes, rules or regulations to the contrary and
12 pursuant to funds allocated to the Third Senatorial District the Saipan and Northern Islands
13 Legislative Delegation (SNILD) hereby appropriates $500,000.00 of the revenues collected for the
14 Third Senatorial District pursuant to Public Law 20-59 as follows:
15 (A) $100,000.00 for the Saipan Mayors Office for disaster recovery due to the extra
16 ordinary circumstances caused by Typhoon Sinlaku (funds maybe utilized for limited
17 term hiring) expenditure authority shall be the Mayor of Saipan or his/her designee;
18 (B) $50,000.00 for the Northern Islands Mayors Office (NIMO) for disaster recovery due
19 to the extra ordinary circumstances caused by Typhoon Sinlaku (funds may be utilized
20 for limited term hiring) expenditure authority shall be Mayor of Northern Islands or
21 his/her designee;

6 SECTION 3. REPROGRAMMING.
7 Funds appropriated under this act shall not be reprogrammed for any other purpose and
8 notwithstanding any provision of law, all funds appropriated under this Act shall be without fiscal
9 year limitation.
10 SECTION 4. REPORTING OF EXPENDITURES.
11 Expenditure authorities of the funds allocated under Section 2 of this Act shall be
12 responsible to report all expenses to the Secretary of the Department of Finance and the
13 Chairperson of the Saipan and Northern Islands Legislative Delegation when funds are fully
14 exhausted.
15 Provided further, that recipients under Sections 2(A) and (B) shall follow the FEMA
16 Incident Command System (ICS) reporting procedures through the CNMI Homeland Security
17 Emergency Management/Emergency Operations Center (EOC). Furthermore, if any funds
18 appropriated under Sections 2(A) and (B) shall be deemed reimbursable by FEMA and/or EOC or
19 both. Reimbursed funds shall be remitted to the Saipan and Northern Islands Legislative
20 Delegation for appropriation purpose(s).
