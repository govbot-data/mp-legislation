Twenty -Fourth Northern Marianas Commonwealth Legislature
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
April 18, 2026
FIRST SPECIAL SESSION, 2026 H. L. B. 24-55
A LOCAL APPROPRIATION BILL FOR AN ACT
FOR THE THIRD SENATORIAL DISTRICT
To appropriate revenues collected for the Third Senatorial District pursuant to
Public Law 20-59.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE THIRD SENATORIAL DISTRICT DELEGATION
PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF THE
COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Delegation finds that Typhoon Sinlaku caused widespread damage
3 across the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands (CNMI), resulting in
4 significant disruption to infrastructure, public services, housing and economic
5 activity. The Delegation finds that Typhoon Sinlaku placed significant strain on
6 residents, including but not limited to displacement of families and overcrowding
7 in temporary shelters, increased health risks due to sanitation challenges and limited
8 access to medical care, loss of income for workers and small business owners and
9 psychological stress and trauma associated with the disaster. Given the magnitude
10 of destruction and the limited fiscal capacity of the CNMI external funding
11 assistance is necessary to ensure and equitable and timely recovery. The Delegation

HOUSE LOCAL BILL 24-55
1 finds that without such support, recovery efforts will be significantly delayed,
2 prolonging hardship for residents and increasing long-term economic losses.
3 The Delegation further finds that Typhoon Sinlaku represents a major
4 disaster with far-reaching consequences. The Delegation finds that immediate and
5 sustained funding assistance is critical to restore essential services and rebuild
6 communities. Furthermore, the Delegation finds that strategic investment at this
7 stage will not accelerate recovery but the funding assistance will reduce
8 vulnerability, protect lives and livelihoods in the immediate future.
9 Therefore, the purpose of this Act is to appropriate local funds from the
10 revenues collected for the Third Senatorial District pursuant to Public Law 20-59.
11 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.
12 Notwithstanding any provision(s) of law, statutes, rules or regulations to the
13 contrary and pursuant to funds allocated to the Third Senatorial District the Saipan
14 and Northern Islands Legislative Delegation (SNILD) hereby appropriates
15 $500,000.00 of the revenues collected for the Third Senatorial District pursuant to
16 Public Law 20-59 as follows:
17 (A) $100,000.00 for the Saipan Mayors Office for disaster recovery due to
18 the extra ordinary circumstances caused by Typhoon Sinlaku (funds
19 maybe utilized for limited term hiring) expenditure authority shall be
20 the Mayor of Saipan or his/her designee;

11 SECTION 3. REPROGRAMMING.
12 Funds appropriated under this act shall not be reprogrammed for any other
13 purpose and notwithstanding any provision of law, all funds appropriated under this
14 Act shall be without fiscal year limitation.
15 SECTION 4. REPORTING OF EXPENDITURES.
16 Expenditure authorities of the funds allocated under Section 2 of this Act
17 shall be responsible to report all expenses to the Secretary of the Department of
18 Finance and the Chairperson of the Saipan and Northern Islands Legislative
19 Delegation when funds are fully exhausted.
20 Provided further, that recipients under Sections 2(A) and (B) shall follow
21 the FEMA Incident Command System (ICS) reporting procedures through the

7 If any provision of this Act or the application of any such provision to any

12 SECTION 6. SAVINGS CLAUSE.
13 This Act and any repealer contained herein shall not be construed as
14 affecting any existing right acquired under contract or acquired under statutes
15 repealed or under any rule, regulation or order adopted under the statutes.
16 Repealers contained in this Act shall not affect any proceeding instituted under or

19 already be in existence on the date this Act becomes effective.
