A LOCAL APPROPRIATION BILL FOR AN ACT
FOR THE THIRD SENATORIAL DISTRICT
To re-appropriate the remaining fund balance of $89,217.00 under
Saipan Local Law 22-15 (Project Code 3754H) for the maintenance and
improvements of Commonwealth parks and recreational facilities in
Precinct III and the maintenance and improvements of Garapan Core
and the Froilan C. Tenorio Beach Road pathway in Precinct III; and for
other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE THIRD SENATORIAL DISTRICT
DELEGATION PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF
THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. PURPOSE.
2 The purpose of this Act is to re-appropriate the remaining fund balance of
3 $89,217.00 under Saipan Local Law 22-15 (Project Code 3754H) for the
4 maintenance and improvements of Commonwealth parks and recreational facilities
5 in Precinct III, and the maintenance and improvements of the Garapan Core and the
6 Froilan C. Tenorio Beach Road pathway in Precinct III and for other purposes.

2 Notwithstanding any provision(s) of law, statutes, rules or regulations to the

6 3754H); as follows:
7 (a) Eighty-Nine Thousand Two Hundred Seventeen dollars ($89,217.00)

9 improvements of Commonwealth parks and recreational facilities in
10 Precinct III and the maintenance and improvements of the Garapan Core
