A HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION
To approve the Mayor of Saipan's request to reinstate and fill
essential, vacant personnel positions within the Office of the Mayor,
Municipality of Saipan, pursuant to Public Law 24-20.
1 WHEREAS, the Twenty-Fourth Northern Marianas Commonwealth
2 Legislature enacted Public Law 24-20, also known as the Revised Appropriations
3 and Budget Authority Act of 2026; and
4 WHEREAS, Public Law 24-20 establishes strict hiring restrictions and
5 personnel cost containment measures for the government of the Commonwealth of
6 the Northern Mariana Islands (CNMI); and
7 WHEREAS, Section 609 of Public Law 24-20 explicitly states that any
8 position that becomes vacant shall be closed out, and that unused funds for such
9 position shall go to operation expenses within that department or agency within 30
10 days upon vacancy unless a request is made to the Legislature for reinstatement of
11 the vacant position, which shall be approved by a joint resolution; and
12 WHEREAS, through MISC. COMM. (House) 24-129, the Mayor of
13 Saipan issued an urgent appeal to immediately reinstate and fill critical operational
