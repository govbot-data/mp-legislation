A LOCAL APPROPRIATION BILL FOR AN ACT
FOR THE SECOND SENATORIAL DISTRICT
To appropriate Fifty-four Thousand Dollars ($54,000.00) from
revenues collected from poker fees in the Second Senatorial District;
and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE SECOND SENATORIAL DISTRICT
DELEGATION PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF
THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. PURPOSE.
2 The purpose of this Act is to appropriate Fifty-four Thousand Dollars
3 ($54,000.00 USD) from revenues collected from poker fees in the Second
4 Senatorial District. The certification of funds was prepared on December 18, 2025
5 by the Resident Department Head of the Department of Finance, Tinian and has
6 been attached as reference. This appropriation of local funds is not part of the FY
7 2026 Budget Act. Therefore, this local appropriation of local funds does not
8 contravene 1 CMC §7401 and 1 CMC §7404(d) or any other provision of the CNMI
9 Budget Act.
10 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATIONS.

HOUSE LOCAL BILL No. 24-
1 Notwithstanding any provision(s) of law, statutes, rules or regulations to the
2 contrary and pursuant to funds allocated to the Second Senatorial District from
3 poker fees, the Tinian and Aguiguan Legislative Delegation hereby appropriates
4 Fifty-four Thousand Dollars ($54,000.00 USD) of the revenues collected for the
5 Second Senatorial District from the poker fees as follows:
6 (A) $42,000.00 allocated to the Mayor of Tinian and Aguiguan. $32,000.00
7 will be allocated for the operations, personnel, professional services and public
8 programs of the Mayor's office and $10,000.00 for the Mayor's discretionary fund.
9 (B) $3,000.00 to the Tinian Municipal Council for public programs and
10 office operations.
11 (C) $4,000.00 to the Tinian and Aguiguan Legislative Delegation for
12 operations.
13 (D) $5,000.00 to the Tinian Casino Gaming Control Commission for
14 personnel and operations.
15 SECTION 3. EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY.
16 The expenditure authority for funds under Section 2 (A) and (D) shall be
17 the Mayor of Tinian and Aguiguan. The expenditure authority for the funds under
18 Section 2 (B) shall be the Chairman of the Tinian Municipal Council. The
19 expenditure authority for the funds under Section 2 (C) shall be the Chairman of
20 the Tinian and Aguiguan Legislative Delegation.

2 Funds appropriated under this act shall not be reprogrammed for any other
