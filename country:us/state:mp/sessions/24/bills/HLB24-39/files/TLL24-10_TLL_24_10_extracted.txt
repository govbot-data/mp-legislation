A LOCAL APPROPRIATION BILL FOR AN ACT

To appropriate Fifty-four Thousand Dollars ($54,000.00) from revenues collected
from poker fees in the Second Senatorial District; and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE SECOND SENATORIAL DISTRICT DELEGATION PURSUANT
TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. PURPOSE.
2 The purpose of this Act is to appropriate Fifty-four Thousand Dollars ($54,000.00 USD)
3 from revenues collected from poker fees in the Second Senatorial District. The certification of
4 funds was prepared on December 18, 2025, by the Resident Department Head of the Department
5 of Finance, Tinian, and has been attached as reference. This appropriation of local funds is not part
6 of the FY 2026 Budget Act. Therefore, this local appropriation of local funds does not contravene
7 1 CMC §7401 and 1 CMC §7404(d) or any other provision of the CNMI Budget Act.
8 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATIONS.
9 Notwithstanding any provision(s) of law, statutes, rules, or regulations to the contrary and
10 pursuant to funds allocated to the Second Senatorial District from poker fees, the Tinian and

1 Aguiguan Legislative Delegation hereby appropriates Fifty-four Thousand Dollars ($54,000.00
2 USD) of the revenues collected for the Second Senatorial District from the poker fees as follows:
3 (A) $42,000.00 allocated to the Mayor of Tinian and Aguiguan. $32,000.00 will be
allocated for the operations, personnel, professional services, and public programs of the Mayor's
5 office, and $10,000.00 for the Mayor's discretionary fund.
6 (B) $3,000.00 to the Tinian Municipal Council for public programs and office operations.
7 (C) $4,000.00 to the Tinian and Aguiguan Legislative Delegation for operations.
8 (D) $5,000.00 to the Tinian Casino Gaming Control Commission for personnel and
9 operations.
10 SECTION 3. EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY.
11 The expenditure authority for funds under Section 2 (A) and (D) shall be the Mayor of
12 Tinian and Aguiguan. The expenditure authority for the funds under Section 2 (B) shall be the
13 Chairman of the Tinian Municipal Council. The expenditure authority for the funds under Section
14 2 (C) shall be the Chairman of the Tinian and Aguiguan Legislative Delegation.
15 SECTION 4. REPROGRAMMING.
16 Funds appropriated under this act shall not be reprogrammed for any other purpose and
17 notwithstanding any provision of law, all funds appropriated under this Act shall be without fiscal
18 year limitation(s).
19 SECTION 5. MANAGEMENT OF FUNDS.
20 The Secretary of Finance is hereby authorized and directed to transfer the sum of Fifty-
21 Four Thousand Dollars ($54,000), or so much thereof as may be necessary, to the Tinian Municipal

RE: Collection of Poker Fees on Tinian
Dear Mayor Aldan,
This is to inform you that Huang Shun Corporation made payment to the Department of Finance
on Tinian in the amount of $108,000.00 for the renewal of nine (9) poker machine licenses. Deposit
dates into the CNMI Treasury account are as follows:
1. September 29, 2025 $36,000.00
2. October 20, 2025 $36,000.00
3. November 26, 2025 $36,000.00
In accordance with Tinian Local Law 23-10, the annual local license fee for Tinian is $54,000.
