A LOCAL BILL FOR AN ACT
FOR THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT
To establish a docking and watercraft fee for derby event participants
utilizing the West and East Docks on Rota; to include vessels, jet
skis, kayaks, and canoes; to exempt bona fide Rota residents and
educational activities; to provide penalties for violations; and to
allocate collected revenues for conservation and community
programs.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT
DELEGATION PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF
THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Rota Legislative Delegation finds that annual fishing derbies and
3 maritime competitions attract visiting participants using various types of watercraft,
4 including vessels, jet skis, kayaks, and canoes. These events contribute positively
5 to Rota' s tourism and economy, but they also place increased strain on the island's
6 docking facilities, launch areas, and marine environment.
7 The purpose of this Act is to establish a fair and reasonable derby-specific
8 fee system to help maintain dock facilities, promote marine safety and

6 "Section 101. Short T itle. This Act may be cited as the 'Rota Derby
7 Docking and Watercraft Fee Act of 2025:
8 Section 102. Definition.
9 (a) "Bona fide resident of Rota" means an individual who has resided in
10 Rota for at least one (1) year immediately before the derby event and holds a valid
11 Rota voter registration, driver's license, or other government-issued proof of
12 residency verified by the Mayor's Office or DLNR.
13 (b) "Derby event" means any organized fishing, boating, or watercraft
14 competition conducted under a valid permit issued by DLNR or the Mayor's
15 Office.
16 (c) "Watercraft" includes all motorized and non-motorized vessels,
17 including but not limited to boats, jet skis, kayaks, and canoes.
18 (d) "Educational purpose" means any government, school, or community-
19 based instructional activity that does not involve competition or commercial gain.

1 Section 103. Applicability. This Act applies exclusively to officially
2 sanctioned derby events or maritime competitions authorized by the Department of
3 Land and Natural Resources (DLNR) or the Office of the Mayor of Rota.
4 It does not apply to educational programs, such as school-based marine
5 training, youth boat activities, or environmental education programs conducted by
6 government agencies, non-profit organizations, or educational institutions.
7 Section 104. Derby Docking and Watercraft Fees.
8 (a) Non-bona fide residents (visiting derby participants) shall be charged
9 the following:
10 1. Ten Dollars ($10.00) per day per motorized vessel;
11 2. Five Dollars ($5.00) per day per jet ski or personal watercraft;
12 3. Three Dollars ($3.00) per day per kayak or canoe.
13 (b) Bona fide residents of Rota (derby participants) shall be charged a one-
14 time fee of Five Dollars ($5.00) per vessel, valid for a maximum of three (3)
15 consecutive days during the derby event.
16 (c) Fees shall be collected and receipted before or during derby registration.
17 (d) Any vessel or watercraft that remains docked, moored, or operated
18 beyond the official derby period without renewal shall be subject to penalties under
19 Section 8 of this Act.
20 Section 105. Exemptions. The following are exempt from payment of the
21 docking or watercraft fees:
