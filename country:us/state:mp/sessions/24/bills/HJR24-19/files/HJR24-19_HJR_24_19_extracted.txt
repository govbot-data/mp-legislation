A HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION
To approve the Governor's request to exempt and fill a critical and
vacant personnel position within the Office of the Governor,
pursuant to Public Law 24-20, Section 602(a).
1 WHEREAS, the Twenty-Fourth Northern Marianas Commonwealth
2 Legislature enacted Public Law 24-20, also known as the Revised Appropriations
3 and Budget Authority Act of 2026; and
4 WHEREAS, Section 602(a) of Public Law 24-20 mandates a freeze on
5 filling new or vacant positions within the Executive Branch, except for positions
6 essential to the delivery of public services; and
7 WHEREAS, Section 602(a) provides a mechanism to exempt critical
8 vacancies from this freeze upon the formal determination and certification of the
9 Governor, subject to approval by the Legislature via a joint resolution; and
10 WHEREAS, the Office of the Governor serves as the central hub for
11 executive operations, crisis management, and the continuous administration of
12 public services throughout the Commonwealth; and

VI -05
HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION
1 WHEREAS, the physical infrastructure, facility systems, and operational
2 safety of the executive complexes require continuous, dedicated upkeep to prevent
3 hazardous working conditions and costly structural degradation; and
4 WHEREAS, a vacancy in the General Maintenance position compromises
5 the daily operations of the executive branch, creates safety vulnerabilities, and
6 hinders the facility support required by administrative staff to deliver services to
7 the public; and
8 WHEREAS, through Governor's Communication 24-165, the Governor
9 has formally certified to the Legislature that a specific vacant personnel position
10 within the Office of the Governor is essential for the delivery of public services;
11 and
12 WHEREAS, the Legislature recognizes that maintaining the operational
13 integrity of the Commonwealth's executive offices is a necessary public purpose
14 that warrants an exemption from the hiring freeze;
15 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the House of
16 Representatives of the Twenty-Fourth Northern Marianas Commonwealth
17 Legislature, the Senate concurring, that the following personnel is essential for the
18 delivery of public service and approved by the Legislature:
19 1 General Maintenance, Office of the Governor — Admin. Services
20 BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Speaker of the House of
21 Representatives and the President of the Senate shall certify and the House Clerk
