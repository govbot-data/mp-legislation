1 Section 1. Findings and Purpose. The Commonwealth Legislature fmds
2 that amending several sections of the Northern Mariana Islands Election Law is
3 necessary to provide clarity, consistency, and efficiency.
4 The Commonwealth Legislature further finds that Public Law 21-34 signed
5 into law on September 22, 2020, suspended certain CNMI elections laws for the
6 CNMI 2020 General Election to mitigate public health risks due to the Covid-19
7 Pandemic; adjusted the early voting time and added more polling places; extended
8 the hours of operations for early voting and election day; authorized curbside voting
9 and procedures; authorized the Commonwealth Election Commission (CEC) to
10 collect absentee ballots 7 days before the general election and 14 days after; gave
11 CEC flexibility to make changes to the election procedures if necessary to mitigate
12 public health risks; and etc. PL 21-34 contained a sunset provision that

1 automatically expired on November 18, 2020, or after the certification of the CNMI
2 2020 General Election Results, whichever was later.
3 The Commonwealth Legislature finds that certain expired amendments
4 proved beneficial and advantageous to the Commonwealth Election Commission.
5 Section 6222, Early Voting, provides that polling places for early voting must
6 remain open from 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. (instead of 4:00 p.m.) on weekdays,
7 Saturdays, and holidays. Polling places may be closed on Sundays at the discretion
8 of the Commonwealth Election Commission, and the Commission may amend
9 voting days and hours as necessary to accommodate emergency early voting. In
10 addition, to ensure the privacy of our voters, Section 6528. Polling Places, is
11 amended to include language stating that "It is unlawful to carry or use electronic
12 devices in the polling places or voting booth areas. Election officials may provide
13 for the collection and return of electronic devices during the time a voter is casting
14 a ballot."
15 The Legislature intends to improve protections for our voters and ensure the
16 Election Commission has the flexibility to carry out its function of ensuring a fair,
17 honest, orderly, and impartial election process.

5 Section 6. Amendment. Title 1, Division 6, Section 6528 of the
6 Commonwealth Code is hereby amended by adding a new subsection (f) to read as
7 follows:
8 "§ 6528. Polling Places: Three-hundred Foot Radius Prohibition
9 of Campaign Activity.
10 -(0 It is unlawful to carry or use electronic devices in the polling
11 places or voting booth areas. Election officials shall collect and return
12 electronic devices while a voter is casting a ballot. No person shall engage
13 in campaign activity, electioneering, or other partisan conduct within three
14 hundred feet of a polling place. The electronic prohibition, however, does
15 not apply to election officials or authorized election personnel acting in the
16 official performance of their duties.
17 (al Any person who violates any provision of this section shall be
18 fined not more than five hundred dollars or be imprisoned for not more than
19 six months, or both. On a second offense or any succeeding offense, the
20 penalty shall be a fine of not more than one thousand dollars or
21 imprisonment for not more than one year, or both."
