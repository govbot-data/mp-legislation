To amend 10 CMC § 12143 to lower the local gambling taxes,
including online gaming.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT
DELEGATION PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF
THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Rota Legislative Delegation (Delegation) of the Twenty-Fourth
3 Northern Marianas Commonwealth Legislature finds that the island of Rota faces
4 unique and ongoing economic challenges due to its small population base, limited
5 private-sector activity, geographic isolation, and reliance on a narrow range of
6 economic opportunities. The Delegation further finds that lawful gaming activities,
7 particularly online gaming conducted from licensed facilities on Rota, as well as
8 land-based casino operations, represent a viable source of employment, private
9 investment, and government revenue that may contribute meaningfully to the
10 island's economic stability.

The Delegation also finds that existing local tax rates imposed on land-
2 based and online gaming casino operations may place an undue financial burden
3 on gaming operators located on Rota and may discourage continued operations,
4 expansion, or future investment. Adjusting the local tax rates applicable to Gross
5 Gaming Revenues generated by Rota Casino Licensees is therefore necessary to
6 ensure the economic viability of such operations while maintaining a consistent
7 revenue stream for the Commonwealth. A reduced and more sustainable tax
8 structure is favorable, will promote regulatory compliance, support long-term
9 business operations, and advance the public interest of the residents of the First
10 Senatorial District.
11 The purpose of this Act is to amend 10 CMC § 12143 to lower the local
12 Gross Gaming Revenue tax on land-based and online gaming activities generated
13 by Rota Casino Licensees to promote economic development, encourage private
14 investment, support employment opportunities, and ensure the continued
15 generation of government revenue within the First Senatorial District.
