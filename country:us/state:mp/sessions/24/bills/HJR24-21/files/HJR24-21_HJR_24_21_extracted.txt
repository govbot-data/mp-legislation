A HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION
To approve the Governor's request to exempt and fill a critical and
vacant personnel position within the Executive Branch, specifically
for the CNMI Office of Veterans Affairs, pursuant to Public Law
24-20, Section 602(a).
1 WHEREAS, the Twenty-Fourth Northern Marianas Commonwealth
2 Legislature enacted Public Law 24-20, also known as the Revised Appropriations
3 and Budget Authority Act of 2026; and
4 WHEREAS, Section 602(a) of Public Law 24-20 mandates a freeze on
5 filling new or vacant positions within the Executive Branch, except for positions
6 essential to the delivery of public services; and
7 WHEREAS, Section 602(a) provides a mechanism to exempt critical
8 vacancies from this freeze upon the formal determination and certification of the
9 Governor, subject to approval by the Legislature via a joint resolution; and
10 WHEREAS, the CNMI Veterans Cemetery serves as the final resting place
11 for our military veterans and their eligible family members; It is a place of honor,
12 dignity, and remembrance that must be maintained in accordance with established

1 cemetery standards to ensure a respectful environment for veterans, their families,
2 and the public; and
3 WHEREAS, a vacancy in the grounds maintenance position compromises
4 the physical infrastructure, facility systems, and operational safety of the Veterans
5 Cemetery that requires dedicated upkeep to prevent costly structural degradation
6 and is necessary to preserve the appearance, safety, and functionality of the
7 cemetery; and
8 WHEREAS, through Governor's Communication 24-185, the Governor
9 has formally certified to the Legislature that the vacant grounds maintenance
10 position within the CNMI Office of Veterans Affairs is essential for the delivery of
11 public services; and
12 WHEREAS, the Legislature finds that honoring our veterans by properly
13 maintaining their dedicated public facilities is a necessary public service that
14 justifies an exemption from the hiring freeze;
15 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the House of
16 Representatives of the Twenty-Fourth Northern Marianas Commonwealth
17 Legislature, the Senate concurring, that the following personnel is essential for the
18 delivery of public service and approved by the Legislature:
19 1 Ground Maintenance, CNMI Office of Veterans Affairs
