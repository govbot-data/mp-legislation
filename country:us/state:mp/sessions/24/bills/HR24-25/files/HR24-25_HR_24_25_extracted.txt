A HOUSE RESOLUTION
To Congratulate and Honor all the women in the Commonwealth as
we celebrate their contributions and achievements during the month
of March 2026 proclaimed as CNMI Women's Month and March
8th as International Women's Day.
1 WHEREAS, in 1977, the United Nation's General Assembly adopted a
2 resolution calling all their member states to declare a day to commemorate
3 women's rights and international peace; in line with this, the Commonwealth of the
4 Northern Mariana Islands proclaim March 2026 as CNMI Women's Month and
5 March 8th, 2026 as International Women's Day; and
6 WHEREAS, in 1979, the United Nations adopted the "Convention of the
7 Elimination of Discrimination against Women" (CEDAW), which provides a
8 mechanism for international governments to commit themselves to providing equal
9 opportunities for women in the political and public life, as well as in the
10 educational, health, and employment realms; and
11 WHEREAS, throughout our nation's history, women have persevered and
12 made sacrifices in the face of adversity, marginalization, and prejudice, all in
13 pursuit of fairness and equal rights in communities around the country; and

HOUSE RESOLUTION 24-
1 WHEREAS, the International Women's Day, is a global day celebrating
2 the social, economic, cultural and political achievements of women; The day also
3 marks a call to action for accelerating gender parity; and
4 WHEREAS, the 2026 theme for International Women's Day is "Give to
5 Gain"; and
6 WHEREAS, Give To Gain emphasizes the power of reciprocity and
7 support. When people, organizations, and communities give generously,
8 opportunities and support for women increase. Giving is not a subtraction, it's
9 intentional multiplication. When women thrive, we all rise; and
10 WHEREAS, we can all give our support to gain advancement for women
11 and girls; As individuals, giving support means calling out stereotypes, challenging
12 discrimination, questioning bias, celebrating women's success, and more. Giving
13 our support to help advance gender equality reinforces a sense of connection and
14 purpose. This creates an important ripple effect for spreading impact; and
15 WHEREAS, International Women's Day is a chance to pay tribute to
16 women throughout the world and to reflect on the progress and achievements made
17 to advance women's equality, celebrate the gains made by women in our society
18 and reflect on the challenges and barriers that women continue to face; and
19 WHEREAS, standing on the shoulders of the heroines who came before
20 them, today's women and girls continue to carry forward the mission of ensuring
21 our daughters have the same opportunities as our sons; and
