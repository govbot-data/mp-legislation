BE IT ENACTED BY THE THIRD SENATORIAL DISTRICT DELEGATION PURSUANT TO
CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. PURPOSE.
2 The purpose of this Act is to appropriate local funds from the revenues collected for the
3 Third Senatorial District pursuant to Public Law 20-59.
4 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.
5 Notwithstanding any provision(s) of law, statutes, rules or regulations to the contrary and
6 pursuant to funds allocated to the Third Senatorial District, the Saipan and Northern Islands
7 Legislative Delegation (SNILD) hereby appropriates $1,571,460.00 of the revenues collected for
8 the Third Senatorial District pursuant to Public Law 20-59 as follows:

1 (A) $400,000.00 to the Commonwealth Election Commission to address the funding
2 shortfall for the upcoming 2026 General Election, the expenditure authority under 2(A) shall be
3 the Chairperson of the Commonwealth Election Commission or his/her designee;
4 (B) $37,000.00 for the CNMI Arts & Culture to meet the Matching & PJAF (Pacific
5 Jurisdiction Artist Funds) grant Distribution requirements, expenditure authority under 2(B) shall
6 be the Secretary of Finance or his/her designee;
7 (C) $63,000.00 to the CNMI Scholarship and Financial Assistance Office for its operations,
8 expenditure authority under 2(C) shall be the Chairperson of the Board or his/her designee.
9 (D) $991,460.12 for the NMISF Retiree 25% pension payment, expenditure authority of
10 2(D) shall be the Secretary of Finance or his/her designee.
11 (E) $10,000.00 for the Chamolinian Cultural Village, Inc., expenditure authority under
12 2(E) shall be the Secretary of Finance or his/her designee;
13 (F) $35,000.00 for the Office of Carolinian Affairs, expenditure authority under 2(F) shall
14 be the Carolinian Advisory Council;
15 (G) $35,000.00 for the Indigenous Affairs Office, expenditure authority under 2(G) shall
16 be the Resident Executive of the Indigenous Affairs Office.
17 SECTION 3. REPROGRAMMING.
18 Funds appropriated under this Act shall not be reprogrammed for any other purpose and
19 notwithstanding any provision of law, all funds appropriated under this Act shall be without fiscal
20 year limitation.

1 SECTION 4. MANAGEMENT OF FUNDS.
2 (A) The Secretary of Finance or each of their respective designee(s) shall regulate and
3 control the expenditure of the funds appropriated by this Act to ensure that appropriated funds are
4 not expended contrary to the law, fiscal management policies, and practices of the Commonwealth.
5 SECTION 5. REPORTING OF EXPENDITURES.
6 Expenditures of the funds allocated under Section 2 of this Act shall be responsible to
7 report all expenses to the Secretary of the Department of Finance by the end of the 2026 calendar
8 year. Provided further that failure to provide all expenses, entities (Government, non-profits
9 entities, etc... excluding specific projects) that were afforded funds under this Act shall not be able
10 to avail to any future funds appropriated by the Saipan and Northern Islands Legislative
11 Delegation.
12 SECTION 6. SEVERABILITY.
13 If any provision of this Act or the application of any such provision to any person or
14 circumstance should be held invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remainder of this Act
15 or the application of its provisions to persons or circumstances other than those to which it is held
16 invalid shall not be affected thereby.
17 SECTION 7. SAVINGS CLAUSE.
18 This Act and any repealer contained herein shall not be construed as affecting any existing
19 right acquired under contract or acquired under statutes repealed or under any rule, regulation or
20 order adopted under the statutes. Repealers contained in this Act shall not affect any proceeding
21 instituted under or pursuant to prior law. The enactment of the Act shall not have the effect of
