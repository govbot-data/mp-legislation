TWENTY-FOURTH NORTHERN MARIANAS COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE
SECOND REGULAR SESSION, 2025 S.R. NO. 24-12
A SENATE RESOLUTION
To commend and congratulate Jacqueline Ayuyu Quitugua for her
exemplary service, leadership, and dedication for more than four decades to
education, student well-being, and youth development in the
Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.
1 WHEREAS, this Legislative Body is pleased to acknowledge stewards of our
2 community who have played a critical role in strengthening the educational system of our
3 islands with an unwavering dedication, innovative efforts, and commitment to the growth
4 and success of the youth of the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands; and
5 WHEREAS, Jacqueline Ayuyu Quitugua was born on the beautiful, pristine island
6 of Rota to proud parents, †Juan Crisostimo and †Maria Manglona Ayuyu, and together
7 with her siblings: †Vicente Ayuyu, †Barbara Ayuyu Barcinas, †Frederick Ayuyu, Rosita
8 Ayuyu Hocog, George Ayuyu, Luis Ayuyu, Juan Ayuyu, were raised with humility, faith,
9 and a deep passion to serve their beloved community; and
10 WHEREAS, Jacqueline Ayuyu Quitugua began her education deeply rooted in
11 faith as an elementary student at Mount Carmel School in Guam, a middle school student
12 at Bishop Baumgartner Memorial Catholic School in Guam, and earned her high school
13 diploma from Notre Dame High School in Talafofo, Guam in 1972; and
14 WHEREAS, Jacqueline Ayuyu Quitugua would follow a journey that led her to
15 obtain a Bachelor of Arts in Education from the University of Guam, in 1979, and after
16 much experience in the education system, she obtained a Masters of Education in
17 Administration and Supervision from the University of Guam, in 1991. Through her
18 determination and focus on her academics, Jacqueline Ayuyu Quitugua, a Phi Delta Kappa
19 member, graduated Cum Laude from the University of Guam; and

1 WHEREAS, Jacqueline Ayuyu Quitugua has devoted over forty years of
2 distinguished service to the education system of the Commonwealth of the Northern
3 Mariana Islands (CNMI), beginning her career as a classroom teacher in 1978 and rising
4 through the ranks to become one of the CNMI Public School System’s (PSS) most
5 respected senior leaders; and
6 WHEREAS, Jacqueline Ayuyu Quitugua currently serves as the Senior Director
7 for Student Support and Services of the CNMI Public School System, where she oversees,
8 coordinates, and facilitates a comprehensive portfolio of student-centered programs
9 including, but not limited to, AmeriCorps, Athletics, Early Intervention, Counseling,
10 Mental Health, Cooperative Education, JROTC, Leadership Corps, PREP, and services for
11 incarcerated youth; and
12 WHEREAS, in this role, Jacqueline Ayuyu Quitugua provides visionary
13 leadership to ensure alignment of all programs with the PSS Strategic Plan, the CNMI
14 Profile of a Graduate, and college and career readiness goals, while promoting equitable
15 access to supportive services that foster academic success, personal growth, and student
16 well-being; and
17 WHEREAS, Jacqueline Ayuyu Quitugua works collaboratively with the
18 Commissioner of Education, the Board of Education, school administrators, program
19 managers, teachers, parents, youth, and community partners, as well as interagency
20 partners including the Departments of Public Health, Youth Services, and Youth Affairs,
21 the Northern Marianas College, and regional Pacific entities; and
22 WHEREAS, Jacqueline Ayuyu Quitugua has demonstrated exceptional expertise
23 in grant writing, fiscal stewardship, and program evaluation, successfully overseeing the
24 proposal, management, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation of numerous federally
25 funded programs that have strengthened instructional quality, student supports, health and
26 wellness initiatives, and youth development services across the CNMI; and
27 WHEREAS, from 2017 to 2024, Jacqueline Ayuyu Quitugua served as Senior
28 Director for Curriculum and Instruction, formerly Associate Commissioner for Curriculum
29 and Instruction, where she led the development, implementation, and assessment of
30 standards-based curriculum and instruction across all core content areas, including
