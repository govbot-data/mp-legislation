4 The Legislature recognizes that Jose Camacho Sablan began his journey in January

11 promoted to Police Captain in September 1989. In March 1991, Jose Camacho Sablan took on
12 the role of Assistant Chief of Police for the Boating Safety Division, where he proudly served
13 the Commonwealth until his retirement in July 1996.

15 certificates and accolades throughout his time with the Department of Public Safety. This not

A. Purpose:

B. Committee Findings:
Your Committee finds that Mr. Jose Camacho Sablan is a retired law enforcement officer
who proudly served alongside his fellow officers in blue under the CNMI Department of
Public Safety (DPS). With nearly 20 years of dedicated service, Mr. Sablan made a lasting
impact with the department during his tenure with the DPS Boating Safety Division. He
was recognized for his resourcefulness, skillful training, and unwavering passion for
serving and protecting those who ventured into the surrounding waters of the
Commonwealth.
Your Committee further fmds that Senate Bill No. 24-38 intends to honor Mr. Sablan's
selfless contributions to the safety and well-being of mariners by renaming the DPS
Boating Safety Building to the "Jose Camacho Sablan Boating Safety Station".
Your Committee met on December 2, 2025, to formally discuss the proposed legislation.
In consideration of the testimonies presented in support of the proposed legislation, during
the Senate Session on July 14,2025, where Senate Bill No. 24-38 appeared on the Agenda,
the renaming of the Boating Safety Building serves as a meaningful tribute to Mr. Sablan,
who has positively impacted the community. Accordingly, your Committee supports the
intent of the proposed legislation and recommends the passage of Senate Bill No. 24-38 in
its current form.
C. Legislative History:
Senate Bill No. 24-38 was formally introduced by Senator Manny Gregory T. Castro on
June 24, 2025, and was subsequently referred to the Senate Standing Committee
on Judiciary, Government, and Law for disposition.
D. Public Hearing and Comment:
1. Public Hearing. No public hearing was scheduled for Senate Bill No. 24-38. However,
a committee meeting, which is open to the public, was held on December 2,2025. See
below for further details.
2. Committee Meeting. The proposed legislation made its first appearance during the
scheduled committee meeting on Tuesday, December 2, 2025. The committee meeting
notices and agenda were officially posted on November 26,2025, on various legislative
forums and social media sites to invite members of the general public to provide
comments as indicated under Item No. IV. Public Comment on the Committee Agenda.
However, no oral testimonies in support or in opposition of the proposed legislation
were presented to your Committee.
3. Written Comment. Your Committee sent out several requests for comments on the
proposed legislation. To date, the status of comments received from the following
government and private entities is as follows:

IL ANALYSIS:
A. Purpose:
The purpose of Senate Bill No. 24-83 is to rename the DPS Boating Safety Building to the
"Jose Camacho Sablan Boating Safety Station."
B. Committee Findings:
Your Committee finds that Jose Camacho Sablan led a longstanding career full of integrity,
purpose, and dedication especially in his service towards the Department of Public Safety. Through
his nearly 20 year long service to the department, he has risen through the ranks to eventually
become Police Captain and later, Assistant Chief of Police for the Boating Safety Division until
retiring in 1996. Throughout the expanse of his dedication, he received numerous, well-deserved
recognitions, certificates, and accolades that highlight his commitment to the Department of Public
Safety and to the people of the CNMI. As a result, your Committee fittingly renames the DPS
Boating Safety Building to "Jose Camacho Sablan Boating Safety Station" to commemorate his
many years of committed public service. Therefore, your Committee supports the intent of the
proposed legislation and also recommends the passage of Senate Bill No. 24-38 in its current form.
C. Public Comments/Public Hearing:
Public comments were not solicited, and no comments were received.
D. Legislative History:
Senate Bill No. 24-38 was formally introduced in the Senate by Senator Manny T. Castro on
June 24, 2025, and subsequently referred to the Senate Standing Committee on Judiciary,
Government and Law for disposition. Senate Bill No. 24-38 was passed on Final Reading by the
Senate on February 4, 2026. Senate Bill 24-38 was then transmitted to the House of
Representatives for action on February 5, 2026. On February 9, 2026, Senate Bill 24-38 was
conferred to the House Standing Comrattee on Judicial and Governmental Operations for
disposition.
E. Cost Benefit:
The enactment of House Bill No. 24-38 will incur minimal costs to the CNMI government to
update the signage to reflect the building's renaming.
