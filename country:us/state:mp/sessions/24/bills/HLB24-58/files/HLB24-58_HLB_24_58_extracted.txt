DELEGATION PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF
THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION I. PURPOSE.
2 The Rota Local Law 24-05 under Section 2(c) reserved $200,000 for
3 appropriation by the Rota Legislative Delegation. See Rota Local Law 24-05,
4 Section 2(c).
5 Professional development and specialized training are necessary to
6 strengthen the Commission's regulatory capacity, improve operational
7 effectiveness, and ensure adherence to applicable laws and regulations governing
8 gaming activities within the Commonwealth. Therefore, the purpose of this bill is
9 to appropriate Fifty Thousand Dollars ($50,000.00) of the funding reserved under
10 Rota Local Law No. 24-05, under Section 2(c) for regulatory and compliance

1 training purposes within the Rota Casino Gaming Commission.
2 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.
3 Notwithstanding any provisions, statutes, laws, or regulations to the
4 contrary, the Rota Legislative Delegation hereby appropriates, from the reserved
5 funds under Rota Local Law 24-05, Section 2(c), Fifty Thousand Dollars
6 ($50,000.00) for the purpose of regulatory and compliance training within the Rota
7 Casino Gaming Commission.
8 SECTION 3. EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY.
9 The expenditure authority of the funding appropriated under Section 2 of
10 this act shall be the Chairperson of the Rota Casino Gaming Commission.
11 SECTION 4. REPORTING.
12 The expenditure authority shall submit detailed monthly reports showing
13 the expenditure of funds appropriated under this Act. These reports shall be
14 furnished to the Chairpersons of the Rota Municipal Council and the First
15 Senatorial District Delegation until all appropriated funds have been fully
16 expended. If the expenditure authority fails to meet this reporting requirement, no
17 further expenditures shall be authorized under this Act until the required reports are
18 submitted.
