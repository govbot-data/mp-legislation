A BILL FOR AN ACT
To promote coordination between the Marianas Visitors Authority and the
Commonwealth Council for Arts and Culture for the promotion of
Commonwealth culture and cultural tourism; to preserve the independence of the
Commonwealth Council for Arts and Culture as the Commonwealth's State Arts
Agency; to establish minimum governance, accountability, and artist-payment
standards; and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE 24TH NORTHERN MARIANAS
COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 Section 1. Short Title. This Act may be cited as the "Marianas Cultural-
2 Tourism Integration Act of 2026."
3 Section 2. Findings and Purpose.
4 The Legislature makes the following findings:
5 The culture, history, traditions, arts, and indigenous heritage of the
6 Commonwealth are essential public resources and important components of the
7 Commonwealth's tourism identity;
8 The Marianas Visitors Authority ("MVA") is authorized under 4 CMC §
9 2103 to promote indigenous arts and crafts, preserve the cultural heritage of the

3 The Commonwealth Council for Arts and Culture ("CCAC") serves as the
4 Commonwealth's designated State Arts Agency for purposes of federal arts
5 programs administered by the National Endowment for the Arts (NEA) and must
6 maintain the independence necessary to satisfy applicable federal requirements;
7 Greater coordination between MVA and CCAC can strengthen cultural
8 tourism, expand opportunities for Commonwealth artists and cultural practitioners,
9 and more effectively present the Commonwealth's cultural heritage to residents and
10 visitors;
11 The Legislature further finds that the continued receipt and responsible
12 administration of federal arts funding requires effective governance, financial

14 corrective action when material deficiencies are identified; and

8 Section 5. Protection of CCAC State Arts Agency Status.
9 (a) CCAC shall remain the Commonwealth's designated State Arts Agency
10 for purposes of applicable federal arts programs.
11 (b) Nothing in this Act shall be construed to merge CCAC into MVA or to
12 transfer CCAC's authority over federally funded arts grants to MVA or any other
13 Commonwealth agency.
14 (c) CCAC shall retain independent authority over the evaluation, selection,
15 approval, and administration of grants to the extent required by applicable federal
16 law and grant conditions.
17 (d) Nothing in this Act shall be construed to supersede Executive Order 94-3
18 except to the extent expressly provided by law.
