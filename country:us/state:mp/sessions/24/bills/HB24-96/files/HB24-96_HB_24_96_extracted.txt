1 Section 1. Findings and Purpose. Tourism is the cornerstone of the
2 Commonwealth's private-sector economy and remains directly dependent on
3 sustained air access, destination competitiveness, and visitor demand. The
4 Legislature finds that, despite stabilization efforts, the tourism sector continues to
5 operate under severe constraints, including airline seat capacity well below pre-
6 pandemic levels, reduced or suspended routes, historically low hotel occupancy
7 outside limited peak periods, and declining visitor arrivals that closely track airlift
8 availability.
9 Ongoing structural and external pressures have materially impeded recovery,
10 including unfavorable exchange rates in core source markets, global aircraft
11 delivery delays, heightened regional competition driven by aggressive subsidy

1 programs, and aviation market uncertainty affecting carrier deployment decisions.
2 The Legislature further finds that existing hotel occupancy levels remain far below
3 estimated break-even thresholds, locally generated revenues such as Hotel
4 Occupancy Tax collections have declined sharply from pre-pandemic levels, and
5 these revenue shortfalls have constrained the scale and timing of tourism recovery
6 efforts.
7 Uninterrupted and reliable air service is the most critical determinant of
8 tourism performance in the Commonwealth, as evidenced by strong load factors
9 despite overall capacity reductions. The Legislature finds that strategic airline
10 incentive support, cooperative marketing programs, and sustained destination
11 branding are necessary to retain existing service, stabilize frequencies, encourage
12 seasonal and future expansion, and signal to airline partners that the
13 Commonwealth is committed to protecting and rebuilding its airlift in a highly
14 competitive regional environment.
15 Responding to these conditions, the purpose of this Act is to appropriate
16 $960,235.74 in Tobacco Settlement Funds, identified by the Governor as additional
17 funds available for appropriation from outside sources, in Governor's
18 Communication (House) 24-103, to the Marianas Visitors Authority for tourism
19 recovery efforts.
