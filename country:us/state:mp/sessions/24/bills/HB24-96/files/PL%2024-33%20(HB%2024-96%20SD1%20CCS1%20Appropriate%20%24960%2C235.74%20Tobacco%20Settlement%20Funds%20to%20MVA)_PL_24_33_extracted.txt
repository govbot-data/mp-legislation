MARIANAS COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 Tourism is the cornerstone of the Commonwealth's private-sector economy
3 and remains directly dependent on sustained air access, destination
4 competitiveness, and visitor demand. The Legislature finds that, despite
5 stabilization efforts, the tourism sector continues to operate under severe
6 constraints, including airline seat capacity well below pre-pandemic levels,
7 reduced or suspended routes, historically low hotel occupancy outside limited peak
8 periods, and declining visitor arrivals that closely track airlift availability.

1 Ongoing structural and external pressures have materially impeded
2 stabilization, including unfavorable exchange rates in core source markets, global
3 aircraft delivery delays, heightened regional competition driven by aggressive
4 subsidy programs, and aviation market uncertainty affecting carrier deployment
5 decisions. The Legislature further finds that existing hotel occupancy levels
6 remain far below estimated break-even thresholds, locally generated revenues
7 such as Hotel Occupancy Tax collections have declined sharply from pre-pandemic
8 levels, and these revenue shortfalls have constrained the scale and timing of
9 tourism stabilization efforts.
10 Uninterrupted and reliable air service is the most critical determinant of
11 tourism performance in the Commonwealth, as evidenced by strong load factors
12 despite overall capacity reductions. The Legislature finds that strategic airline
13 incentive support, cooperative marketing programs, and sustained destination
14 branding are necessary to retain existing service, stabilize frequencies, encourage
15 seasonal and future expansion, and signal to airline partners that the
16 Commonwealth is committed to protecting and rebuilding its airlift in a highly
17 competitive regional environment.
18 Responding to these conditions, the purpose of this Act is to appropriate
19 $960,235.74 in Tobacco Settlement Funds, identified by the Governor as
20 additional funds available for appropriation from outside sources, in Governor's
21 Communication (House) 24-103, to the Marianas Visitors Authority for tourism
22 stabilization efforts.

1 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.
2 Notwithstanding any laws, rules, or regulations to the contrary, the Tobacco
3 Settlement Funds in the amount of $960,235.74 is hereby appropriated as follows:
4 (a) $960,235.74 shall be allocated to the Marianas Visitors Authority for
5 tourism stabilization efforts. The expenditure authority shall be the Chairperson
6 of the Board of Directors of the Marianas Visitors Authority. Provided however
7 that $70,000.00 each for the First and Second Senatorial Districts shall be used
8 for stabilization efforts.
9 SECTION 3. NO FISCAL LIMITATION.
10 The funds appropriated under Section 2 shall be without fiscal year
11 limitation.
12 SECTION 4. SEVERABILITY.
13 If any provisions of this Act or the application of any such provision to any
14 person or circumstance should be held invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction,
15 the remainder of this Act or the application of its provisions to persons or
16 circumstances other than those to which it is held invalid shall not be affected
17 thereby.
18 SECTION 5. SAVINGS CLAUSE.
19 This Act and any repealer contained herein shall not be construed as
20 affecting any existing right acquired under contract or acquired under statutes
21 repealed or under any rule, regulation, or order adopted under the statutes.
22 Repealers contained in this Act shall not affect any proceeding instituted under or
23 pursuant to prior law. The enactment of the Act shall not have the effect of
24 terminating, or in any way modifying, any liability, civil or criminal, which shall
25 already be in existence on the date this Act becomes effective.
