LEGISLATURE
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

A HOUSE RESOLUTION
Requesting and directing that the Commonwealth Utilities
Corporation (CUC) undergo a comprehensive, independent, third-
party audit by qualified utility expert auditors, including financial,
operational "desk," asset, compliance, and related examinations;
requiring Commonwealth Public Utilities Commission (CPUC)
coordination, formal CPUC docketing and public process for audit
review; requiring public reporting, a corrective action plan, a
ratepayer impact matrix, a public-facing audit implementation
dashboard, and an immediate 90-day control-fix priority list.
1 WHEREAS, the Commonwealth Utilities Corporation (CUC) is a public
2 utility responsible for the provision of essential electric, water, and wastewater
3 services, and the reliability and affordability of these services directly impact public
4 health, economic stability, and quality of life within the Commonwealth; and
5 WHEREAS, sound utility governance necessitates accurate financial
6 reporting, complete and verifiable asset records, effective internal controls, prudent
7 procurement and contract management, and transparent performance metrics; and
8 WHEREAS, the Commonwealth Public Utilities Commission (CPUC)
9 holds regulatory responsibilities related to public utility oversight and consumer
10 protection, serving as a key entity for receiving, reviewing, and acting upon

3 WHEREAS, independent third-party utility audits are particularly crucial
4 for confirming the integrity of utility financial information that can influence
5 ratepayer charges, validating asset condition and capital planning which affect
6 system reliability and long-term costs, and testing billing, collections, procurement,
7 and inventory controls that can materially impact customer bills and public
8 confidence; and
9 WHEREAS, a comprehensive audit should, at a minimum, encompass an
10 examination of financial practices, cash management, accounts receivable and
11 collections, accounts payable, payroll, fuel and power cost processes, procurement
12 and contract administration, inventory management, capital project controls,
13 system operations, and include a verified asset inventory and asset management
14 program; and
15 WHEREAS, the Legislature has a fundamental duty to ensure transparency
16 and accountability in the administration of public services and in the diligent
17 stewardship of public resources; and
18 WHEREAS, it is deemed essential for the Commonwealth Utilities
19 Corporation (CUC) to obtain and complete a comprehensive independent third-
20 party utility audit, to be performed by qualified outside expert auditors with
21 demonstrated experience in auditing electric and water/wastewater utilities, in

3 WHEREAS, the credibility and efficacy of such an audit necessitate that
4 the audit team be selected through a competitive procurement process and meet
5 rigorous minimum requirements, including strict independence from CUC, its

16 operational "desk audit" and management systems review, a comprehensive asset
17 audit and verification, and a robust compliance and risk review, including the
18 identification of high-risk control gaps and operational/financial exposures; and
19 WHEREAS, to ensure transparency, accountability, and public
