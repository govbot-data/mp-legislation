TWENTY-FOURTH NORTHERN MARIANAS COMMONWEALTH
LEGISLATURE
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
SESSION, 2025 H.L.B. 24-
A LOCAL BILL FOR AN ACT
FOR THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT
To appropriate $16,925 of the Rota Poker License Fee Account under
BU 3302; and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT
DELEGATION PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF
THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Office of the Secretary of Finance certified that the balance of the Rota
3 Poker Fees, BU 3302, is $16,925 as of September 30, 2025. The Rota Legislative
4 Delegation needs to fund its operations and assist in funding municipal events and
5 activities.
6 Therefore, the purpose of this Act is to appropriate $16,925.00 under
7 Business Unit 3302.

HOUSE LOCAL BILL 24-
1 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.
2 Notwithstanding 10 CMC § 1901, $16,925.00 of the Poker License Fee
3 Account, Business Unit 3302, is appropriated to fund the Rota Legislative
4 Delegation operations and assist in funding municipal events and activities.
5 SECTION 3. EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY.
6 The expenditure authority is the Chairperson of the Rota Legislative
7 Delegation.
8 SECTION 4. SEVERABILITY.
9 Suppose any provision of this Act or the application of any such provision
10 to any person or circumstance should be held invalid by a court of competent
11 jurisdiction. In that case, the remainder of this Act or the application of its
12 provisions to persons or circumstances other than those to which it is held invalid
13 shall not be affected thereby.
14 SECTION 5. SAVINGS CLAUSE.
15 This Act and any repealer contained herein shall not be construed as
16 affecting any existing right acquired under contract or acquired under statutes.
17 Repealers contained in this Act shall not affect any proceeding instituted under or
18 pursuant to prior law. The enactment of the Act shall not have the effect of
19 terminating, or in any way modifying, any liability, civil or criminal, which shall
20 already be in existence on the date this Act becomes effective.
