SECOND REGULAR SESSION, 2025 H. L. B. NO. 24-35
A LOCAL APPROPRIATION BILL FOR AN ACT
FOR THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT
To appropriate $16,925 of the Rota Poker License Fee Account under BU 3302; and
for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT DELEGATION PURSUANT TO
CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Office of the Secretary of Finance certified that the balance of the Rota Poker Fees,
3 BU 3302, is $16,925 as of September 30, 2025. The Rota Legislative Delegation needs to fund its
4 operations and assist in funding municipal events and activities.
5 Therefore, the purpose of this Act is to appropriate $16,925.00 under Business Unit 3302.
6 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.
7 Notwithstanding 10 CMC § 1901, $16,925.00 of the Poker License Fee Account, Business
8 Unit 3302, is appropriated to fund the Rota Legislative Delegation operations and assist in funding
9 municipal events and activities.

Rota Local Law No. 24-06
HOUSE LOCAL BILL No. 24-35
1 SECTION 3. EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY.
2 The expenditure authority is the Chairperson of the Rota Legislative Delegation.
3 SECTION 4. SEVERABILITY.
4 Suppose any provision of this Act or the application of any such provision to any person
5 or circumstance should be held invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction. In that case, the
6 remainder of this Act or the application of its provisions to persons or circumstances other than
7 those to which it is held invalid shall not be affected thereby.
8 SECTION 5. SAVINGS CLAUSE.
9 This Act and any repealer contained herein shall not be construed as affecting any existing
10 right acquired under contract or acquired under statutes. Repealers contained in this Act shall not
11 affect any proceeding instituted under or pursuant to prior law. The enactment of the Act shall not
12 have the effect of terminating, or in any way modifying, any liability, civil or criminal, which shall
13 already be in existence on the date this Act becomes effective.
14 SECTION 6. EFFECTIVE DATE.
15 This Act shall take effect upon its approval by the Governor or it becoming law without
16 such approval.
