A HOUSE RESOLUTION
To honor, commend, and memorialize the life and legacy of the late
Honorable Diego Tenorio Benavente for his distinguished public
service, steadfast leadership, athletic and maritime
accomplishments, and enduring contributions to the Commonwealth
of the Northern Mariana Islands; and to extend the Legislature’s
deepest condolences and prayers to his wife, children,
grandchildren, friends, and the entire Benavente family.
1 WHEREAS, the Honorable Diego Tenorio Benavente was born on April 21,
2 1959, in the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands to proud parents
3 †Roman Manahane Benavente and †Dolores Tenorio Benavente; and
4 WHEREAS, he was a proud graduate of Mount Carmel School, where he
5 first developed the values of faith, service, and community that shaped his
6 exemplary life; and in 2002, his alma mater honored him with the Distinguished
7 Graduate Award in recognition of his achievements and continued support for
8 Catholic education; and
9 WHEREAS, the late Honorable Diego Tenorio Benavente dedicated his life
10 to serving the people of the Commonwealth with integrity, humility, and

3 WHEREAS, his distinguished public service began with his election in 1990
4 to the Seventh Northern Marianas Commonwealth Legislature, representing Saipan
5 Election District II, and continued through the Twelfth Legislature; during this
6 time, he served as Vice Speaker of the Eighth Legislature and as Speaker of the
7 Ninth, Tenth, and Eleventh Legislatures, guiding landmark measures that
8 strengthened the Commonwealth’s economy, infrastructure, and education; and
9 WHEREAS, in 2002 he was elected Lieutenant Governor of the
10 Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, serving honorably under
11 Governor Juan N. Babauta until 2006; and afterwards returned to the House of
12 Representatives as a member of the Sixteenth and Seventeenth Legislatures, where
13 he chaired key committees and served as Minority Leader; and
14 WHEREAS, upon his retirement from the Legislature on July 16, 2011, after
15 more than nineteen years of elected service, Mr. Benavente was widely recognized
16 as one of the Commonwealth’s most experienced and respected statesmen; and
17 WHEREAS, his public service extended beyond the Commonwealth into
18 regional affairs, including his service as Honorary Consul of the Federated States
19 of Micronesia to the CNMI beginning in 2018, his role in establishing the Pacific
20 Islands Development Bank (PIDB), and his long-standing involvement with the
21 Association of Pacific Island Legislatures (APIL), through which he strengthened
