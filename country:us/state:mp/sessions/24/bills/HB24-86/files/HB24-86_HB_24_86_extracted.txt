LEGISLATURE
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

A BILL FOR AN ACT
To amend the Operator's License Fees as codified under 9 CMC
§ 2202 to establish a 50% discount on all operator's license fees
for CNMI government retirees, senior citizens, persons with
disabilities, and military veterans with disabilities; and for other
purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE 24TH NORTHERN MARIANAS
COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 Section 1. Findings: The Legislature finds that operator's license fees
2 impose a financial burden on certain vulnerable and deserving populations within
3 the Commonwealth, including senior citizens, persons with disabilities, military
4 veterans with service-connected disabilities, and CNMI government retirees living
5 on fixed incomes. These individuals have contributed significantly to the
6 Commonwealth through public service, military service, or perseverance in the face
7 of significant health and mobility challenges.
8 The Legislature further finds that many jurisdictions offer reduced or no-
9 fee operator licensing options for these groups, thereby promoting mobility,
10 independence, and access to essential services. Reducing operator's license fees for

1 these individuals is consistent with the Commonwealth's values of fairness, respect,
2 and support for those who have served their communities and the country.
3 Therefore, the purpose of this Act is to amend existing law to provide CNMI
4 government retirees, senior citizens, individuals with disabilities, and military
5 veterans with disabilities a 50% discount on operator's license fees, which include
6 the initial issuance, renewal, and replacement.
