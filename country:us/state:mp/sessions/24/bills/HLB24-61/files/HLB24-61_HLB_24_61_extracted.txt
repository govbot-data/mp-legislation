A LOCAL APPROPRIATION BILL FOR AN ACT
FOR THE SECOND SENATORIAL DISTRICT
To appropriate Forty-two thousand dollars ($42,000.00) from revenues
collected from poker fees in the Second Senatorial; and for other
purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE SECOND SENATORIAL DISTRICT
DELEGATION PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF
THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. PURPOSE.
2 The purpose of this Act is to appropriate Forty-two thousand dollars
3 ($42,000.00) from revenues collected from poker fees in the Second Senatorial
4 District. The certification of funds was prepared on August 6, 2026 by the Acting
5 Resident Department Head of the Department of Finance, Tinian and has been
6 attached as reference. This appropriation of local funds is not part of the FY 2026
7 Budget Act. Therefore, this local appropriation of local funds does not contravene
8 1 CMC §7401 and 1 CMC §7404(d) or any other provision of the CNMI Budget
9 Act.
10 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.

12 Funds appropriated under this act shall not be reprogrammed for any other
13 purpose and notwithstanding any provision of law, all funds appropriated under this
14 Act shall be without fiscal year limitation(s).
