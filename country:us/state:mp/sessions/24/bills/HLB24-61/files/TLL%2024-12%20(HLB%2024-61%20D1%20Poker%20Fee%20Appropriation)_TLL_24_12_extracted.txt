BE IT ENACTED BY THE SECOND SENATORIAL DISTRICT DELEGATION PURSUANT
TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
SECTION 1. PURPOSE.
2 The purpose of this Act is to appropriate Forty-two thousand dollars ($42,000.00) from
3 revenues collected from poker fees in the Second Senatorial District. The certification of funds
4 was prepared on August 6, 2026, by the Acting Resident Department Head of the Department of
5 Finance, Tinian, and has been attached as reference. This appropriation of local funds is not part
6 of the FY 2026 Budget Act. Therefore, this local appropriation of local funds does not contravene
7 1 CMC §7401 and 1 CMC §7404(d) or any other provision of the CNMI Budget Act.
8 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.
9 Notwithstanding any provision(s) of law, statutes, rules, or regulations to the contrary and
10 pursuant to funds allocated to the Second Senatorial District from poker fees, the Tinian and

1 Aguiguan Legislative Delegation hereby appropriates Forty-two thousand dollars ($42,000.00) of
2 the revenues collected for the Second Senatorial District from the poker fees as follows:
3 (A) $17,250.00 shall be allocated to the Office of the Mayor of Tinian and Aguiguan for
4 operations, personnel, professional services, and public programs.
5 (B) $10,000.00 shall be allocated to the Mayor of Tinian" s Discretionary Fund.
6 (C) $2,000.00 shall be allocated to the 2nd Combined Law Enforcement Academy
7 graduation activities.
8 (D) $12,750.00 shall be allocated to the Tinian Casino Gaming Control Commission:
9 (1) $6,750.00 for the compensation of current and former commissioners;
10 (2) $6,000.00 for the personnel and operations of the TCGCC.
11 SECTION 3. EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY.
12 The expenditure authority for funds under Section 2(A)-(D) shall be the Mayor of Tinian
13 and Aguiguan.
14 SECTION 4. REPROGRAMMING.
15 Funds appropriated under this Act shall not be reprogrammed for any other purpose, and
16 notwithstanding any provision of law, all funds appropriated under this Act shall be without fiscal
17 year limitation(s).
18 SECTION 5. MANAGEMENT OF FUNDS.
19 The Secretary of Finance is hereby authorized and directed to transfer the sum of Forty-
20 two thousand dollars ($42,000.00), or so much thereof as may be necessary, to the Tinian
21 Municipal Treasurer for the local public purposes specified in this Act. This transfer shall not be
