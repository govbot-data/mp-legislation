A HOUSE RESOLUTION
Commending and Honoring Gloria Worswick Hunter for her
historic two decades of cabinet leadership as Special Assistant for
Programs and Legislative Review, her lifelong devotion to public
service, and her enduring legacy as a cultural matriarch of the
Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.
1 WHEREAS, the Legislature proudly honors and recognizes those
2 exemplary individuals whose dedicated public service, outstanding achievements
3 and altruism enhance the quality of life within the Commonwealth; and
4 WHEREAS, Gloria Worswick Hunter came to the Marianas in the early
5 1960s, carrying rich heritage from Angaur, Palau, and Yap, Federated States of
6 Micronesia, graduated from Mount Carmel High School on Saipan in 1967, and
7 chose Saipan as her permanent home, successfully building bridges of
8 understanding, inclusivity, and camaraderie among diverse Pacific communities;
9 and
10 WHEREAS, prior to her career in public administration, Gloria Worswick
11 Hunter was recruited by Abed Younis and served as a reporter for the island's main
12 newspaper, the Marianas Variety, starting in 1978, contributing to local journalism

1 and community reporting during a pivotal period in the Commonwealth's early
2 political development; and
3 WHEREAS, prior to her appointment to the Governor's cabinet, Gloria
4 Worswick Hunter demonstrated early administrative leadership, beginning her
5 legislative service as a clerk during the First Northern Marianas Commonwealth
6 Legislature and subsequently serving as Legislative Affairs Officer (LAO) during
7 the Second Northern Marianas Commonwealth Legislature (1980-1981), in which
8 she managed official legislative communications, acknowledged Senate
9 resolutions, and coordinated liaison affairs between administrative offices and
10 lawmakers; and
11 WHEREAS, Gloria Worswick Hunter subsequently dedicated her life to
12 exemplary executive public service within the Commonwealth of the Northern
13 Mariana Islands (CNMI), serving with distinction as the Special Assistant for
14 Programs and Legislative Review (SAPLR) in the Office of the Governor across a
15 remarkable period of five consecutive four-year terms from January 1982 to
16 January 2002; and
17 WHEREAS, as the longest-serving cabinet-level official in CNMI
18 administrative history, Gloria Worswick Hunter demonstrated unparalleled
19 professional neutrality, administrative skill, and institutional dedication, seamlessly
20 bridging executive policy across three distinct gubernatorial administrations under
21 both major political parties; and

15 regional "Cultural Beauty Icon" and a leading advocate for cultural preservation,
16 serving as a vital repository of Micronesian traditional craftwork, culinary
17 knowledge, indigenous wisdom, and regional history, thereby enriching the cultural
18 tapestry of the CNMI; and
19 WHEREAS, affectionately known as "Mom" or "Auntie" throughout the

22 in the Commonwealth; and

16 preservation, and her irreplaceable legacy as a matriarch of the Northern Mariana
17 Islands; and
