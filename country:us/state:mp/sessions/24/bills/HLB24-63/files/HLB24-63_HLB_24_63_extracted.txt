A LOCAL APPROPRIATION BILL FOR AN ACT
FOR THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT
To appropriate $16,000 of the Rota Poker License Fee Account under BU
3302; and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT DELEGATION
PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF THE
COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Office of the Secretary of Finance certified that the balance of the Rota Poker
3 Fees, BU 3302, is $16,627.50 as of July 31, 2026. The Rota Legislative Delegation finds
4 it important to provide funds for the Rota Patients' Medical Subsistence Allowance.
5 Therefore, the purpose of this Act is to appropriate $16,000.00 under Business Unit
6 3302.
7 SECTION 2. APPROPRIATION.
8 Notwithstanding 10 CMC § 1901, $16,000.00 of the Poker License Fee Account,
9 Business Unit 3302, is appropriated to fund the Rota Patients' Medical Subsistence
10 Allowance.
11 SECTION 3. EXPENDITURE AUTHORITY.
12 The Secretary of Finance shall be the expenditure authority.
