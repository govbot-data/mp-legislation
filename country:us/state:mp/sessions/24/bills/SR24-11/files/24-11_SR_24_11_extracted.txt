TWENTY-FOURTH NORTHERN MARIANAS COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE
SECOND REGULAR SESSION, 2025 S.R. NO. 24-11
A SENATE RESOLUTION
To honor the distinguished service of Chief Warrant Officer Four
Rosannie Castro McGee upon her retirement from the United States
Army, and to commend her exemplary leadership, unwavering, and
outstanding contributions to the defense of our nation.
1 WHEREAS, this Legislative Body is proud to recognize and honor the sons and
2 daughters of the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands who have answered the
3 call to serve in the United States Armed Forces with courage, commitment, and distinction,
4 protecting our nation’s security, and proudly representing our island community; and
5 WHEREAS, Chief Warrant Officer Four (CW4) (Ret.) Rosannie Castro McGee
6 was born and raised on the beautiful island of Saipan to proud parents Joseph M. Castro
7 and Julita Castro. Alongside her siblings—Amy T. Castro, Gregorie Ike C. Castro, Dolorie
8 Lynn C. Rawley, Joelie C. Castro, Remedio C. Castro, and Joseph C. Castro—she was
9 instilled with the values of respect, hard work, and a deep sense of service; and
10 WHEREAS, CW4 (Ret.) McGee began her education at Mount Carmel School
11 from 1985-1995, before relocating to Washington state, where she graduated from
12 Spanaway Lake High School in 1998. She continued her education at Pierce College, from
13 1999-2000, after which she made the selfless decision to pursue a military career; and
14 WHEREAS, after Quartermaster training in 1998, CW4 (Ret.) McGee served as a
15 unit supply specialist with the 380th CONUS Replacement Center Battalion and 959th
16 Replacement Company; and
17 WHEREAS, in 2003, CW4 (Ret.) McGee supported Operation Enduring Freedom
18 at Fort Bliss, Texas, facilitating logistical coordination between the military and UNICOR;

1 WHEREAS, as the S4 NCOIC of the 476th Chemical Battalion in 2005, CW4
2 (Ret.) McGee managed forecasting and equipment accountability during deployment and
3 redeployment operations for units within the battalion as a Staff Sergeant; and
4 WHEREAS, promoted to the rank of Sergeant First Class in 2008, CW4 (Ret.)
5 McGee became an instructor at the Army Readiness Training Academy, developing
6 training for the Army’s first web-based logistics system; and
7 WHEREAS, later that same year, CW4 (Ret.) McGee attended Warrant Officer
8 Candidate School and subsequently served as Property Book Officer for both the 483rd
9 and 385th Transportation Battalions (TC BN), overseeing integrated logistics and
10 maintaining 100% accountability of equipment; and
11 WHEREAS CW4 (Ret.) McGee maintained comprehensive property
12 accountability initiatives and oversaw equipment modernization programs while serving
13 with the 961st Engineer Battalion in 2013 and the 450th TC BN in 2015; and
14 WHEREAS, during her assignment with the 654th Regional Support Group in
15 2017, CW4 (Ret.) McGee played a key role in implementing the Army's second web-based
16 logistics and fiscal management application. This initiative advanced the Army Chief
17 Information Officer modernization goals and supported the objectives of the Identity,
18 Credential and Access Management program; and
19 WHEREAS, from 2020 until her retirement in 2025, CW4 (Ret.) McGee continued
20 to support the Army’s mission by training and mentoring unit commanders within the 457th
21 TC BN to strengthen property accountability and supply readiness; and
22 WHEREAS, CW4 (Ret.) McGee’s extensive military education includes Primary
23 Leadership Development Course (2001), Basic Non-Commission Officer Course (2005),
24 Battle Staff Non-Commission Officer (2006), Army Basic Instructor Course (2008),
25 Warrant Officer Candidate School (2008), Warrant Officer Basic Course (2009), Property
26 Accounting Technician Warrant Officer Advance Course (2015), and the Quartermaster
27 Warrant Officer Intermediate Level Education Follow-on Course (2020); and
28 WHEREAS, CW4 (Ret.) McGee’s exemplary service has been recognized with
29 numerous awards, including the Meritorious Service Medal (four awards), Army
