A HOUSE RESOLUTION
To recognize, honor, and express appreciation to Rufina Tuloi Borja

education.
1 WHEREAS, the Legislature recognizes that selfless service and steadfast
2 dedication to educating and uplifting our youth—exemplified by those who devote
3 their lives to teaching, mentoring, and fostering academic and personal growth-
4 serve as a guiding light, inspiring each of us to cultivate our talents and contribute
5 wholeheartedly to the prosperity of the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana
6 Islands (CNMI); and
7 WHEREAS, Rufina Tuloi Borja Seman has devoted 50 years to the noble
8 profession of education, demonstrating unwavering commitment to the intellectual,
9 social, and emotional growth of countless students; and
10 WHEREAS, she began her distinguished career with 5 years of service at
11 Mans Stella Elementary School in Palau, where she established a strong foundation
12 for young learners and inspired a lifelong love of learning; and
