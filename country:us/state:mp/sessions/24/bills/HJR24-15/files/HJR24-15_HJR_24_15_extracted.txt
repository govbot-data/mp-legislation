A HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION
To sincerely recognize and express deepest appreciation to Master
Navigator Sesario Sewralur, honoring his extraordinary life, his
unwavering dedication to preserving traditional Micronesian
navigation, and his lasting contributions to the cultural heritage of
the people of Micronesia, and to extend heartfelt condolences to his
family and friends.
1 WHEREAS, this Legislative Body acknowledges the indelible impact of
2 individuals who have committed their lives to the enthusiastic preservation and
3 transmission of cultural and traditional practices, enriching the lives of the people
4 of our Commonwealth; and
5 WHEREAS, this Legislative Body, representing the people of the
6 Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, pays tribute to the late Master
7 Navigator Sesario Sewralur, who devoted his life to preserving, practicing, and
8 passing on the sacred traditions of Micronesian navigation, ensuring that the
9 ancestral knowledge entrusted to him will continue to guide future generations; and
10 WHEREAS, under the guidance and mentorship of his father, the
11 renowned tMaster Navigator Pius "Mau" Piailug, Sesario Sewralur embraced the
