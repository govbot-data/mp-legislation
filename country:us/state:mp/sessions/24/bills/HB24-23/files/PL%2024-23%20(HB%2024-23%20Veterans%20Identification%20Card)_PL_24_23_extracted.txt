1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Legislature finds that the current requirements for obtaining a
3 Veterans License Plate should be amended to provide veterans with additional
4 options for proving their eligibility. While the DD214 (Certificate of Release or
5 Discharge from Active Duty) has been the primary document for verification,
6 many veterans now possess a Veteran ID Card (VIC) issued by the
7 U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. Since obtaining a VIC requires a DD214,
8 the VIC serves as official proof of veteran status and should be accepted as an

1 alternative form of veteran status verification in certain contexts. In particular,
2 the Legislature finds that the VIC should be accepted as an alternative form of
3 verification in the context of applying for a Veterans License Plate in the CNMI.
4 This bill also updates the definitions of "veteran" and "disabled veteran" to
5 explicitly include individuals who served in a reserve component of any branch of
6 the United States Armed Forces. Additionally, it removes the specific listing of
7 each military branch in favor of a broader reference to the Armed Forces. This
8 change ensures the law remains adaptable by allowing for the seamless inclusion
9 of newly established branches, such as the United States Space Force, without
10 requiring future legislative amendments.
11 Thus, based on the foregoing, the purpose of this Act is to amend
12 9 CMC § 2116 to update the definitions of "veteran" and "disabled veteran" and
13 to allow valid VICs be used as proof of veteran status when applying for a
14 veteran's license plate. This amendment ensures that eligible veterans can more
15 easily access the benefits and recognition they deserve.
16 SECTION 2. AMENDMENT.

19 unchanged and in full force and effect, as follows:
