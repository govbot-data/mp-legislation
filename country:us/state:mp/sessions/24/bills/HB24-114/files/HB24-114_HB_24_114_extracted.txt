A BILL FOR AN ACT
To amend the CNMI Coral Reef Protection Act as codified
under 2 CMC, Division 1, Chapter 6, Article 6; and for other
purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE 24TH NORTHERN MARIANAS
COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 Section 1. Findings and Purpose: The Legislature finds that CNMI
2 Public Law 20-79, codified under 2 CMC § 1661 et seq., entitled, "Coral Reef
3 Protection Act", was enacted to protect coral reefs through timely and efficient
4 recovery from damage resulting from natural or manmade activities. Cognizant that
5 existing protections require strengthening to address gaps in implementation,
6 enforcement, and recovery, the Legislature finds that it is appropriate to amend
7 Public Law 20-79 to expand protections for CNMI coral reefs by broadening the
8 definition of harm to coral reef resource, safeguarding the Coral Reef Restoration
9 Fund to address the Commonwealth's future needs, ensuring standardized
10 enforcement and appeals procedures, and for other purposes.
11 Section 2. Amendments. 2 CMC, Division 1, Chapter 6, Article 6. Coral
12 Reef Protection Act is hereby amended to read as follows:
