A HOUSE RESOLUTION
To honor and commend the employees and crews of the
Commonwealth Utilities Corporation and the Guam Power
Authority for their courage, technical expertise, and dedicated
service in restoring power to the Commonwealth following Super
Typhoon Sinlaku and other natural disasters.
1 WHEREAS, the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands has
2 endured the destructive force of numerous natural disasters, including typhoons,
3 super typhoons, tropical storms, flooding, damaging winds, and other emergencies
4 that have severely impacted homes, businesses, public facilities, critical
5 infrastructure, and the daily lives of our people; and
6 WHEREAS, Super Typhoon Sinlaku once again reminded the people of
7 the Commonwealth how fragile island infrastructure can be, and how essential
8 reliable electricity is to public safety, healthcare, water production, wastewater
9 operations, communications, food preservation, fuel access, schools, businesses,
10 and the overall recovery of our community; and
11 WHEREAS, in the aftermath of such disasters, when families are securing
12 their homes, checking on loved ones, clearing debris, and facing uncertainty, the
13 employees and crews of the Commonwealth Utilities Corporation (CUC) answered
