A HOUSE RESOLUTION
To deeply honor and celebrate the impactful life and indelible legacy
of the late Honorable Benigno Magofna Sablan, whose tireless
dedication and myriad contributions were instrumental to the
progress of the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands and
to humbly offer our heartfelt condolences to his bereaved family.
1 WHEREAS, this Legislative Body recognizes that the prosperity and
2 character of life within our esteemed Commonwealth are profoundly strengthened
3 by the steadfast commitment and altruistic efforts of remarkable citizens who
4 dedicate themselves to public service; and
5 WHEREAS, it is with deep respect that this Legislative Body, representing
6 the citizens of the Northern Mariana Islands, gathers today to honor the memory
7 and celebrate the remarkable life and public service of the late Honorable Benigno
8 Magofna Sablan, whose significant contributions and exemplary work have greatly
9 elevated the quality and well-being of our community; and
10 WHEREAS, the late Honorable Benigno M. Sablan, affectionately known
11 as "Tan Ninu," was born on February 27, 1946, on Saipan, the cherished son of the
12 late Mr. Jose Repeki Sablan and Mrs. Clara Magofna Sablan; and

9 WHEREAS, the extensive and diverse career of the late Honorable
10 Benigno M. Sablan began in 1964 when he served as a Junior Clerk for Medical
11 Services in Saipan; and
12 WHEREAS, the late Honorable Benigno M. Sablan served as an
13 Elementary School Teacher for the Department of Education in Saipan and Tinian
14 from 1966 to 1970; and
15 WHEREAS, the late Honorable Benigno M. Sablan contributed
16 significantly to marine conservation as a Master Diver for the Starfish Control
17 Program in Saipan (1970), the Marshall Islands (1970-1972), and the Department
18 of Marine Resources in Kolonia, Pohnpei (1972-1973), a role that involved
19 eradicating the infestation of the crown-of-thorns starfish and collecting marine and
20 estuary specimens for scientific study; and
