1 Section 1. Findings and Purpose. The Legislature finds that the CNMI
2 Women's Association has been an essential non-profit office whose vision is that
3 women are fully engaged in the political, economic, and social development of the
4 Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands (CNMI.) As a part of its mission
5 to uplift and empower the women of the CNMI, this Legislative body deems it
6 fitting to formally name the CNMI Women's Association Center in honor of the
7 late Honorable Felicidad Leiwamal Taman Ogumoro.
8 The late Honorable Felicidad Leiwamal Taman Ogumoro was a paragon of
9 altruistic public service and generous community enrichment throughout her
10 illustrious life, especially for the women of the CNMI. Examples of her
11 distinguished career as a public servant spans many years. In 1978, the late
12 Honorable Felicidad Ogumoro became the first woman of Carolinian heritage to be
