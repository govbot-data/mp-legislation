A LOCAL BILL FOR AN ACT
FOR THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT
To amend the Wedding Cake Mountain Wildlife Conservation Area,
to authorize the Mayor of Rota to plan, construct, and maintain a
pedestrian pathway at Wedding Cake Mountain and access to the
Grotto, and to initiate actions necessary to qualify for federal grants,
and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE FIRST SENATORIAL DISTRICT
DELEGATION PURSUANT TO CHAPTER 4, DIVISION 1, TITLE 1 OF
THE COMMONWEALTH CODE:
1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Rota Legislative Delegation finds that Rota Local Law 9-3 established
3 the Wedding Cake Mountain Wildlife Conservation Area to protect wildlife, plant
4 life, soils, and the scenic coastal environment of the Taipingot Peninsula on Rota.
5 Over the years, public use of the Wedding Cake area and nearby natural attractions,
6 including the Grotto, has increased. Unmanaged foot traffic has the potential to
7 damage fragile habitat, accelerate erosion, and increase risks to visitor safety.

14 a pathway at Wedding Cake Mountain and access to the Grotto, in a manner
