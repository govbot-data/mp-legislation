AN ACT
To AUTHORIZE THE CONTINUED PAYMENT OF EMPLOYER PREMIUMS
FOR THE GROUP HEALTH & LIFE INSURANCE (GHLI)
PROGRAM BY PERMITTING EXPENDITURE OF $3,747,585.00
RECENTLY IDENTIFIED BY THE GOVERNOR THAT SHALL BE
APPROPRIATED BY THE LEGISLATURE FOR FY 2026.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE TWENTY-FOURTH NORTHERN
MARIANAS COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Legislature finds that the Governor, in a communication dated
3 November 14, 2025, notified the presiding officers that the Group Health & Life
4 Insurance (GHLI) Program is at immediate risk of termination due to the absence
5 of funding for the GHLI Retiree business unit (11881700) in the Fiscal Year 2026
6 Budget. The Governor reported that approximately $7.2 million is required to
7 maintain uninterrupted retiree employer premium payments and avoid the
8 cancellation of retiree coverage.

Public Law No. 24-19
HOUSE BILL 24-75
1 The Legislature further finds that the Governor identified $3,747,585.00 in
2 available revenues for Fiscal Year 2026. These revenues can be immediately
3 applied toward urgent government priorities, including constitutional obligations
4 and the protection of critical programs such as GHLI.
5 The Legislature additionally finds that Article XV, Section 1(e) of the
6 Commonwealth Constitution mandates that twenty-five percent (25%) of all
7 general revenues shall be appropriated to the Public School System. Consistent
8 with this constitutional requirement, $936,896.25, representing twenty-five
9 percent (25%) of the identified revenues, must be allocated to the Public School
10 System for Fiscal Year 2026 operations.
11 The Legislature further finds that after satisfying the constitutional
12 allocation to the Public School System, the remaining seventy-five percent (75%)
13 of the identified revenues—$2,810,688.75—may be used to sustain the GHLI
14 Program. These funds are immediately needed to support the GHLI Retiree
15 business unit (11881700) and prevent termination of retiree coverage.
16 Accordingly, the purpose of this Act is to authorize the Secretary of Finance
17 to utilize $3,747,585.00 in Fiscal Year 2026 revenues as follows:
18 (1) $936,896.25 to the Public School System in compliance with Article XV,
19 Section 1(e) of the Commonwealth Constitution; and
20 (2) $2,810,688.75 for allocation into and expenditure under GHLI Retiree
21 business unit 11881700 for retiree employer premium payments.
2

1 SECTION 2. ENACTMENT.
2 Notwithstanding any laws, rules, or regulations to the contrary, the
3 Secretary of Finance is hereby authorized to utilize $3,747,585.00 in Fiscal Year
4 2026 revenues as follows:
5 (a) $936,896.25 shall be allocated and expended for the Public School System
6 in compliance with Article XV, Section 1(e) of the Commonwealth Constitution;
7 and
8 (b) $2,810,688.75 shall be allocated into and expended under the GHLI
9 Retiree business unit (11881700) exclusively for the payment of retiree employer
10 premiums.
11 This authorization does not constitute a new appropriation and does not
12 increase the Fiscal Year 2026 budget ceiling.
13 SECTION 3. SEVERABILITY.
14 If any provisions of this Act or the application of any such provision to any
15 person or circumstance should be held invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction,
16 the remainder of this Act or the application of its provisions to persons or
17 circumstances other than those to which it is held invalid shall not be affected
18 thereby.
19 SECTION 4. SAVINGS CLAUSE.
20 This Act and any repealer contained herein shall not be construed as
21 affecting any existing right acquired under contract or acquired under statutes
22 repealed or under any rule, regulation, or order adopted under the statutes.
