1 SECTION 1. PURPOSE.
2 The purpose of this Act is to appropriate up to One Hundred Thousand Dollars

6 Notwithstanding any provisions, statutes, laws, or regulations to the contrary, the Saipan
7 and Northern Islands Legislative Delegation hereby appropriates up to One Hundred Thousand

9 Public Law 20-59 as follows:
10 (A) $100,000.00 is hereby appropriated as follows:
