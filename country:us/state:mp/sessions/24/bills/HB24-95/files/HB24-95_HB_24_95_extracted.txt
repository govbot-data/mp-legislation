A BILL FOR AN ACT
To enhance economic opportunities for small businesses and
local farmers by establishing new gross revenue tax exemptions,
clarifying export treatment, simplifying tax administration in the
CNMI; providing for phased implementation to ensure
administrative feasibility and fiscal stability; and for other
purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE 24TH NORTHERN MARIANAS
COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 Section 1. Findings and Purpose. The Legislature finds that small
2 businesses, including emerging "pop-ups," micro-businesses, and local farmers, are
3 critical drivers of the Commonwealth's economy, providing essential goods,
4 services, and employment opportunities. Many of these enterprises operate on
5 limited revenue margins, and the burden of certain taxes, coupled with complex
6 filing requirements, disproportionately impacts their viability. This burden can
7 impede their ability to grow, expand, and reinvest in their operations, thereby
8 limiting overall economic diversification and resilience within the Commonwealth.
9 The Legislature finds that allowing small businesses and local farmers to
10 retain a greater portion of their earnings will encourage them to reinvest in their

2 stimulate local economic circulation. Therefore, it is the intent of this Act to provide
3 targeted economic relief and simplify administrative processes for eligible small

5 contribute more robustly to the Commonwealth's prosperity. The Legislature

12 The Legislature finds that it has come to the attention of the CNMI
13 Legislature that the Commonwealth Department of Revenue and Taxation has
14 misconstrued the taxation imposed on goods produced or manufactured in the
15 Commonwealth solely for export to destinations outside of the Commonwealth. In
16 this time of economic hardship, as the Commonwealth attempts to attract new
17 foreign investment with the creation of a Free Trade Zone and the offering of
18 various tax incentives, the actions taken by the Department of Revenue and
19 Taxation have the potential to damage investment attraction efforts. Notably, the
20 Department has imposed double taxation on companies producing and
21 manufacturing goods for export, by assessing both the Gross Revenue Tax of 4

3 Section 4. Enactment. Notwithstanding any laws or regulations to the
4 contrary and subject to proper codification by the CNMI Law Revision
5 Commission, under Title 4, Division 1, Chapter 3, a new Section 1314 is hereby
6 enacted:
7 "§ 1314. Fiscal Impact Statement and Sustainability Review.
8 (a) Fiscal Impact Statement. Prior to the effective date of any tax rate
9 changes or new exemptions detailed in this Act, the Department of Finance
10 shall prepare and submit to the Legislature a comprehensive fiscal impact
11 statement, projecting the estimated revenue loss or gain over the next five
12 (5) fiscal years. This statement shall include an assessment of the potential
13 impacts on the Commonwealth's budget and essential public services.
14 (b) Sustainability Review. The Legislature shall review the fiscal
15 impact statement and assess the Commonwealth's fiscal capacity to absorb
16 the revenue changes proposed by this Act. Based on this review, the
17 Legislature may, by subsequent concurrent resolution, adjust the phased
18 implementation timelines or specific tax rates/exemptions as deemed
19 necessary to maintain fiscal stability."
