A BILL FOR AN ACT
To amend 3 CMC § 1205, relating to the Chamorro and
Carolinian Language Policy Commission's authority to assess
fees for translation services; to establish a revolving fund; and
for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE 24TH NORTHERN MARIANAS
COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 Section 1. Findings and Purpose. The Legislature finds that the
2 Chamorro and Carolinian Language Policy Commission bears the crucial mandate
3 of promoting and preserving the Chamorro and Carolinian languages, ensuring
4 their continued vitality and use within the Commonwealth. The fulfillment of this
5 mandate requires consistent and substantial funding to support essential programs
6 · and activities. However, the reliance solely on annual budget appropriations creates
7 financial instability, potentially hindering the Commission's ability to effectively
8 execute its core responsibilities and long-term strategic goals.
9 The Legislature further finds that granting the Commission specific
10 authorization to charge fees for translation services and other related operations is
11 a fiscally reasonable step that will considerably improve the Commission's financial

1 independence and sustainability. Establishing a designated revolving fund for these
2 fees will provide a steady revenue foundation for the Commission's long-term
3 operational needs, allowing it to more effectively carry out its objective of
4 preserving and promoting the Chamorro and Carolinian languages for future
5 generations.
6 Section 3. Amendment. 3 CMC § 1205 is hereby amended to read as
7 follows:
8 "§ 1205. Chamorro and Carolinian Language Policy Commission:
9 Duties and Functions.
10 (a) Study antecedents, describe the grammar, and prescribe good usages for

12 (b) Prepare a modern and up-to-date Chamorro-Carolinian-English
13 dictionary;
14 (c) Represent the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands in
15 meetings and conferences on matters dealing with the Chamorro and Carolinian
16 languages;
17 (d) Make recommendations to the Board of Education on standard
18 orthography to be used in the public school system;
19 (e) Research the Chamorro and Carolinian languages and the people, and
20 provide such resources to the Joeten-Kiyu Public Library;

1 (f) Establish, formulate, and enforce policies for government and private
2 sectors regarding Chamorro and Carolinian translations, and to certify all Chamorro

4 (g) Promulgate rules and regulations for Chamorro and Carolinian

12 activities, and promulgate rules and regulations, including a schedule of fees for the

14 fees collected shall be deposited into a revolving fund account established by the
15 Secretary of Finance and designated as the CCLPC Revolving Fund. The CCLPC
16 Revolving Fund shall be non-lapsing and shall be used exclusively for sustaining
17 the activities and functions of the Commission. The expenditure authority of the
18 CCLPC Revolving Fund shall be the Director of the Commission. Fund monies
19 shall be available for expenditure without further appropriation and without fiscal
20 year limitations."
