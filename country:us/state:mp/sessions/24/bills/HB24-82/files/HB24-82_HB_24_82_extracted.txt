TWENTY-FOURTH NORTHERN MARIANAS COMMONWEALTH
LEGISLATURE
IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
Regular Session, 2025 H. B. 24- St
A BILL FOR AN ACT
To amend Section 704(k) of Public Law 24-20 to prevent
disruption or delay of allotments, payments, or expenditures of the
Legislative Branch by adding a transition period of up to 270 days
for the implementation of the Legislature's Finance Department;
and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE 24TH NORTHERN MARIANAS
COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 Section 1. Findings and Purpose. The Legislature finds that Public Law 24-
2 20, Revised Appropriations and Budget Authority Act of 2026, establishes the
3 Legislature Operations Fund and vests expenditure authority in the Director of the
4 Legislative Bureau, thereby fundamentally restructuring how legislative funds are
5 administered and disbursed. The Legislature further finds that implementation of
6 this structure requires independent banking, accounting, payroll, procurement, and
7 reporting systems that do not presently exist within the Legislative Bureau.
8 The Legislature finds that immediate execution of these requirements,
9 without a transition period or implementation funding, would result in the
10 suspension or delay of legislative payments, including payroll, member allowances,
11 vendor payments, and other lawful obligations of the Legislature. Preventing

HOUSE BILL 24- St
1 disruption of these payments is essential to maintaining continuity of legislative
2 operations and avoiding harm to employees, members, vendors, and governmental
3 functions.
4 The Legislature finds that the Department of Finance has acknowledged that
5 a transition of this scale typically requires between ninety and one hundred eighty
6 days and that continued use of existing financial systems during a transition period
7 is necessary to ensure uninterrupted payment processing and audit compliance.
8 The purpose of this Act is to amend Section 704(k) of Public Law 24-20 to
9 prevent disruption or delay of allotments, payments, or expenditures of the
10 Legislative Branch by adding a transition period up to 270 days for the
11 implementation of the Legislature's Finance Department solely to prevent the
12 stoppage or delay of legislative payments, to allow sufficient time for the
13 Legislature to enact corrective legislation, authorize necessary funding, hire
14 personnel, and establish compliant financial systems, while preserving fiscal
15 accountability and continuity of government operations.
16 Section 2. Amendment. The following amendment to Section 704(k) of
17 Public Law 24-20 is hereby enacted into law:
18 Subsection 106. Implementation/Gradual 270-day Transition
19 Period. Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the
