1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Legislature finds that Public Law 24-20, Revised Appropriations and
3 Budget Authority Act of 2026, establishes the Legislature Operations Fund and
4 vests expenditure authority in the Director of the Legislative Bureau, thereby
5 fundamentally restructuring how legislative funds are administered and
6 disbursed. The Legislature further finds that implementation of this structure
7 requires independent banking, accounting, payroll, procurement, and reporting
8 systems that do not presently exist within the Legislative Bureau.
9 The Legislature finds that immediate execution of these requirements,

1 without a transition period or implementation funding, would result in the
2 suspension or delay of legislative payments, including payroll, member
3 allowances, vendor payments, and other lawful obligations of the Legislature.
4 Preventing disruption of these payments is essential to maintaining continuity of
5 legislative operations and avoiding harm to employees, members, vendors, and
6 governmental functions.
7 The Legislature finds that the Department of Finance has acknowledged
8 that a transition of this scale typically requires between ninety and one hundred
9 eighty days and that continued use of existing financial systems during a
10 transition period is necessary to ensure uninterrupted payment processing and
11 audit compliance.
12 The purpose of this Act is to amend Section 704(k) of Public Law 24-20 to
13 prevent disruption or delay of allotments, payments, or expenditures of the
14 Legislative Branch by adding a transition period up to 273 days for the
15 implementation of the Legislature's Finance Department solely to prevent the
16 stoppage or delay of legislative payments, to allow sufficient time for the
17 Legislature to enact corrective legislation, authorize necessary funding, hire
18 personnel, and establish compliant financial systems, while preserving fiscal
19 accountability and continuity of government operations.
20 SECTION 2. AMENDMENT.
21 The following amendment to Section 704(k) of Public Law 24-20 is hereby
22 enacted into law:
23 "Subsection 106. Implementation/Gradual 273-Day Transition
24 Period. Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Department of
25 Finance, working collaboratively with the CNMI Legislative Bureau and
