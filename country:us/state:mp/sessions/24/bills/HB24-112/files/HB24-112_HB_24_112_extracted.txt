A BILL FOR AN ACT
To establish the Disaster Readiness and Recovery Fund; to plan
for the local share of federal disaster assistance; to require an
inventory of public buildings and their insurance; to strengthen
emergency sheltering plans; to require quarterly reports on
federal disaster funds; and for other purposes.

BE IT ENACTED BY THE 24T NORTHERN MARIANAS
COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 Section 1. Short Title. This Act shall be cited as the "Commonwealth
2 Disaster Readiness Act of 2026".
3 Section 2. Findings and Purpose.
4 (a) The Legislature finds that the Commonwealth depends heavily on
5 federal disaster assistance after typhoons and other disasters, and that the people of
6 the Commonwealth are still waiting for the closeout of recovery projects from
7 Super Typhoon Yutu in 2018 while recovering from Typhoon Sinlaku and Super
8 Typhoon Bavi in 2026.
9 (b) The Legislature further finds that the final report of the President's
10 Council to Assess the Federal Emergency Management Agency, issued May 7,
11 2026, recommends that the States and territories carry a larger share of disaster

1 costs, and recommends that higher federal cost shares be tied to local readiness,
2 including a cash reserve equal to 5 percent of the state budget, a disaster reserve or
3 relief fund that can be used for the local match and for events that are not federally
4 declared, insurance on past permanent work, and an emergency operations plan that
5 is reviewed every year.
6 (c) The Legislature further finds that the Commonwealth Code does not
7 now provide a dedicated reserve for the local share of federal disaster assistance or
8 for emergency costs before federal reimbursement arrives. One CMC § 20146(b)
9 provides an account for the operations of the Commonwealth Homeland Security
10 and Emergency Management office, but not a disaster reserve.
11 (d) Whatever form federal reform finally takes; the Commonwealth is
12 better served by preparing now.
13 The Legislature further finds that the purpose of this Act is to build, step by
14 step and within the Commonwealth's means, the local readiness that protects our
15 families, keeps recovery moving, and positions the Commonwealth for the
16 strongest possible federal partnership.
17 Section 3. Enactment. Subject to codification by the CNMI Law Revision
18 Commission, the following provision is hereby enacted:
19 "Commonwealth Disaster Readiness Act of 2026."
20 "§ 101. Definition. As used in this Act:

4 declared by the President of the United States for the Commonwealth.
5 (b) "Federal disaster assistance" means assistance provided under the

7 federal law providing disaster response, recovery or mitigation funds to the

11 (d) "General Fund resources" means the total General Fund resources

13 (e) "Local share" means the portion of the cost of a federally assisted
14 disaster project or program that the Commonwealth must pay under federal law,
15 after any waiver that applies.
16 (f) "Special Assistant" means the Special Assistant for Homeland Security

18 § 102. Disaster Readiness and Recovery Fund.
19 (a) There is established in the Department of Finance a special account to
20 be known as the Disaster Readiness and Recovery Fund. The Secretary of Finance

1 shall keep the Fund separate from the General Fund and from all other accounts,
2 and shall account for all deposits and expenditures separately.
3 (b) Money in the Fund shall not lapse at the end of a fiscal year, and interest
4 earned on the Fund shall be credited to the Fund.
5 (c) The Fund shall not be counted as a resource available for general
6 appropriation in any annual budget act, and shall not be used for any purpose other
7 than those stated in Section 104 of this Act.
8 § 103. Deposits and Reserve Goal.
9 (a) The Fund shall consist of: (1) amounts appropriated to the Fund by the
10 Legislature; (2) federal reimbursements received for costs that were first paid from
11 the Fund, to the extent permitted by federal law; (3) grants, gifts and donations
12 made to the Fund; and (4) interest earned on the Fund.
13 (b) The goal of the Commonwealth is a Fund balance equal to 5 percent of
14 General Fund resources. To reach that goal over time, each annual budget proposal
15 submitted by the Governor shall recommend a deposit to the Fund of not less than
16 1 percent of General Fund resources until the balance equals 5 percent, unless the
17 Governor certifies in the budget proposal that a smaller deposit is necessary and
18 states the reasons.
19 (c) Nothing in this Act reduces, redirects or impairs any revenue that is
20 earmarked or dedicated by existing law, any pledge to bondholders, or any payment

3 § 104. Uses of the Fund.
4 (a) Money in the Fund is appropriated for, and may be spent only for, the
5 following purposes:
6 (1) paying the local share of federal disaster assistance;
7 (2) emergency sheltering, feeding, water, power, and medical
8 support for residents during a declared emergency;
9 (3) advancing cash for costs that are eligible for federal disaster
10 assistance while federal reimbursement is pending, provided that the
11 reimbursement is returned to the Fund when received; and
12 (4) response and recovery costs for a declared emergency that does
13 not receive a federal declaration.
14 (b) Before any expenditure, the Special Assistant and the Secretary of
15 Finance shall jointly certify in writing to the Governor that the expenditure is for a
16 purpose stated in subsection (a). The Governor shall approve each expenditure.
17 (c) Within five working days after any expenditure from the Fund, the
18 Secretary of Finance shall notify the Speaker of the House of Representatives and
19 the President of the Senate in writing of the amount, the purpose, and the declared
20 emergency or federal project involved.

4 § 105. Planning for the Local Share.
5 (a) Each annual budget proposal submitted by the Governor shall include a
6 line item titled "Public Assistance Matching" that states the estimated local share
7 for all open federal disaster projects during the coming fiscal year, together with
8 the recommended source of funding.
9 (b) The Governor's Authorized Representative for federal disaster
10 assistance shall provide the estimate to the Office of Management and Budget no
11 later than sixty days before the budget proposal is due, listed by disaster and by
12 project.
13 § 106. Inventory of Public Buildings and Insurance.
14 (a) Within 180 days after this Act takes effect, the Secretary of Finance,
15 with the Secretary of Public Works and the Governor's Authorized Representative,
16 shall deliver to the Legislature an inventory of public buildings and major public
17 facilities of the Commonwealth government.
18 (b) The inventory shall list, for each building or facility: its location and
19 use; its estimated replacement value; whether it is insured, the type and amount of
20 coverage and the cost of the coverage; whether it was repaired or rebuilt with

3 (c) The report shall also state the estimated cost of obtaining the insurance
4 that federal law requires as a condition of past federal disaster assistance, and the

6 § 107. Emergency Sheltering and Readiness Plan.
7 (a) Within 180 days after this Act takes effect, the Special Assistant shall
8 review the Commonwealth emergency operations plan and deliver to the Governor
9 and the Legislature an emergency sheltering and readiness plan for Saipan, Tinian,
10 Rota and the Northern Islands.
11 (b) The plan shall identify shelters and their capacity; the supplies of food,
12 water, fuel and medicine to be kept on each island; the arrangements for
13 communication and power when off-island help is delayed; the roles of the Mayors,
14 the Public School System and nonprofit partners; and the estimated cost of
15 sheltering if federal support for sheltering is reduced.
16 (c) The Special Assistant shall review and update the plan every year and
17 report the update to the Legislature.

16 available federal share.
