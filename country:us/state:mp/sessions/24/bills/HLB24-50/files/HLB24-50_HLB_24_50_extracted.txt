9 SECTION 3. REPROGRAMMING.
10 Funds appropriated under this act shall not be reprogrammed for any other
11 purpose and notwithstanding any provision of law, all funds appropriated under this
12 Act shall be without fiscal year limitation.
13 SECTION 4. REPORTING OF EXPENDITURES.
14 Expenditures of the funds allocated under Section 2 (B) of this Act shall be
15 responsible to report all expenses to the Secretary of the Department of Finance and
16 the Chairperson of the Saipan and Northern Islands Legislative Delegation by the
17 end of the 2026 calendar year. Provided further that failure to provide all expenses,
18 entities (Government, non-profits entities, etc... excluding specific projects) that
19 were afforded funds under this Act shall not be able to avail to any future funds
20 appropriated by the Saipan and Northern Islands Legislative Delegation.
