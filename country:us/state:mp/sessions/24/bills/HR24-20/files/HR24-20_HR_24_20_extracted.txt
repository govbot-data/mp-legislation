A HOUSE RESOLUTION
To respectfully recognize the significant contributions of Victorino
"Vic" Sablan Cepeda to the educational and vocational
advancement of the people of the Commonwealth of the Northern
Mariana Islands.
1 WHEREAS, the Legislature is firmly committed to honoring those who
2 have demonstrated consistent, whole-hearted dedication to the educational and
3 professional progress and improvement of the people of the Commonwealth of the
4 Northern Mariana Islands; and
5 WHEREAS, Victorino "Vic" Sablan Cepeda is an exemplary individual
6 whose life's work illustrates an earnest passion for uplifting the livelihoods of our
7 local community; and
8 WHEREAS, Mr. Cepeda began his illustrious career as an educator as a
9 fourth-grade teacher in William S. Reyes (WSR) Elementary School, and as an
10 instructor at Oleai Elementary School for three years; and
11 WHEREAS, Mr. Cepeda was later sent by the then Department of
12 Education of the Trust Territory government to teach on the island of Pagan for one
13 year, and a year after his return to Saipan, was sent to the Pohnpei Micronesia

1 Teacher Education Center by the territory government's education department.
2 Afterwards, he taught at the then Hopwood Intermediate School/ Junior-Senior
3 High School; and
4 WHEREAS, Mr. Cepeda was one of the Marianas High School's (MHS)
5 pioneer educators as a mathematics teacher in 1969 when MHS became the first
6 full-fledged public high school to serve the island community during the Trust
7 Territory of the Pacific during the post-war era before later becoming vice
8 principal; and
9 WHEREAS, Mr. Cepeda deepened his involvement in MHS by becoming
10 the acting principal from 1973 to 1979; and
11 WHEREAS, Mr. Cepeda furthered his education in Oregon to strengthen
12 his skills as a leader in education, and upon successful completion and return to the
13 island, became principal again from 1985 until his retirement in 1988; and
14 WHEREAS, Mr. Cepeda is befittingly recognized as the longest serving
15 school administrator in Marianas High School's 55-year history; and
16 WHEREAS, during Mr. Cepeda's tenure in MHS, he implemented key
17 strategies to motivate student learning by encouraging communication and good
18 relationships with the parents by allowing parents to be present when failing
19 students performed their make-up examinations, instituting a buddy system to
20 partner student leaders with students who need assistance or a friend, and
21 administering compulsory attendance of students and parents to parent orientation,
