1 Section It. Findings and Purpose. The Legislature recognizes that in 2009,
2 P.L. 16-48 established the Office of Grants Management, which was subsequently
3 amended in 2016 by P.L. 19-49 to enhance oversight and responsibilities regarding
4 federal grant assistance within the CNMI Goverment. The office that was
5 repurposed has been given the new name CNMI Office of Grants Management &
6 State Clearinghouse (OGM-SC).
7 The Legislature finds that the CNMI OGM-SC shall be permitted the carry-
8 over of funds from year to year with the approval of the Office of the Governor.
9 The Legislature further finds that by amending the current law (1 CMC § 2893(b))
10 to allow the CNMI OGM-SC to carry over funds without the approval of the Office
11 of the Governor could offer several potential benefits, primarily centered on
12 increasing the office's operational efficiency, timeliness, and autonomy in

1 managing federal funds. In addition, it will improve its ability to manage long-term
2 grants and projects, address budget flexibility, and respond to unexpected demands
3 like disasters or pandemics. This would allow the CNMI OGM-SC to secure larger
4 grants, have funds available for community projects beyond annual budget cycles,
5 and manage fluctuations in revenue from federal grants. Giving the Administrator
6 direct expenditure authority over cany-over funds would provide greater
7 autonomy, which will foster a stronger sense of direct responsibility and
8 accountability within the CNMI OGM-SC for those specific funds. Automating or
9 removing the executive approval step for carry-overs standardizes the process,
10 potentially reducing the administrative burden on both the OGM-SC and the
11 Governor's office. This allows both entities to focus resources on other critical
12 governmental functions.
