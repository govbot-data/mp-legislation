1 Section 1. Short Title. This Act shall be cited as the "Business Integrity
2 and Regulation of Tourism Harms Act" or the "BIRTH Act."
3 Section 2. Findings and Purpose. Birthright citizenship belongs to those
4 born on United States soil under the Constitution and long-settled law. Birth
5 tourism, by contrast, is a compensated commercial practice in which travel for the
6 primary purpose of giving birth is promoted, organized, or facilitated for profit, and
7 it is a practice that must not be condoned. The wrong addressed by this Act lies not
8 in the birth of a child in the Commonwealth, but in the profiteering, exploitation,
9 and material deception that drive commercialized birth tourism. Those practices
10 must be stopped, but not at the expense of the citizenship rights that attach to those
11 born in the Commonwealth on United States soil. Accordingly, this Act targets the
12 commercial machinery of birth tourism without compromising the
13 Commonwealth's inherent character as United States soil.

1 Immigration admission, parole, visa issuance, nationality, and citizenship at
2 birth remain matters of federal law, not Commonwealth law. This Act does not
3 adjudicate, alter, narrow, expand, or otherwise affect those federal determinations.
4 Instead, it rests on the Commonwealth's authority to define offenses, regulate
5 businesses, punish fraud and deceptive commercial practices, protect consumers,
6 safeguard public health and safety, regulate housing and licensing, and prevent the
7 laundering or concealment of unlawful proceeds. The Commonwealth cannot
8 correct one wrong by diminishing a constitutional birthright, and this Act therefore
9 targets unlawful commercial conduct rather than the status of any child.
10 Recent public discussion has underscored the need for the Commonwealth
11 to respond with precision, restraint, and effective local enforcement. The official
12 record reflects that the Section 902 consultation process relevant to EVS-TAP was
13 invoked in 2018, continued in 2019, and resulted during the Trump administration
14 in a final recommendation to create EVS-TAP as a restricted sub-program of the
15 Guam-CNMI Visa Waiver Program, with later federal rulemaking implementing
16 that framework. The Legislature therefore finds that EVS-TAP originated in the
17 Trump administration. That history shows that lawful federal screening tools and
18 strong Commonwealth enforcement may operate together, and that the proper local
19 response is to punish compensated commercial facilitation and material deception
20 rather than weaken birthright citizenship.

1 The Commonwealth retains authority to regulate businesses, licensing,
2 consumer protection, public health and safety, nuisances, housing uses, and other
3 commercial conduct within its jurisdiction. That authority includes the power to
4 prohibit compensated commercial arrangements that facilitate birth tourism, punish
5 materially false or deceptive representations made in furtherance of such
6 arrangements, regulate maternity housing used for such arrangements, and recover
7 proceeds derived from unlawful conduct. It also includes the power to distinguish
8 between enterprise principals and lesser facilitators, impose criminal penalties and
9 civil remedies, and preserve safe harbors for lawful medical care, ordinary
10 commerce, legal representation, and unpaid family assistance.
11 Honoring both the Constitution and the Commonwealth's duty to protect its
12 people, the purpose of this Act is to prohibit and deter compensated commercial
13 birth tourism facilitation within the Commonwealth, strengthen local enforcement
14 against those who organize, finance, manage, market, or materially facilitate such
15 conduct for profit, protect limited public resources, preserve the integrity of lawful
16 commerce, and ensure that the Commonwealth addresses abuse through lawful
17 local enforcement directed at the scheme rather than the child and focused on
18 punishing crime rather than diminishing the rights, status, and protections secured
19 to the Commonwealth under the Covenant and the Constitution.

3 "§ 101. Definitions. For purposes of this Act, the following
4 terms shall have the meanings set forth below unless the context
5 clearly requires otherwise:
6 (a) "Birth tourism" means a compensated commercial
7 arrangement under which a person travels to the Commonwealth, or
8 travel is arranged on that person's behalf, for the primary purpose of
9 giving birth in the Commonwealth, where the arrangement includes
10 one or more facilitation or support services and is promoted,
11 organized, or carried out for profit.
12 (b) "Birth tourism business" means a commercial enterprise
13 that, for compensation or the expectation of compensation,
14 knowingly markets, arranges, coordinates, brokers, manages,
15 finances, or provides facilitation or support services in support of
16 birth tourism.
17 (c) "Facilitation or support services" include:
18 (1) arranging or providing lodging for pregnant clients;
19 (2) coordinating transportation, medical appointments, or
20 translation services;
21 (3) providing childbirth-related travel packages or bundled

4 § 102. Prohibition on Commercial Birth Tourism.

6 manage, direct, organize, or finance a birth tourism business within
7 the Commonwealth.

15 maternity housing in furtherance of birth tourism;
16 (3) assist, advertise, or solicit clients for a birth tourism
17 business or offer the coordinated facilitation of travel to the
18 Commonwealth for the primary purpose of giving birth here;
19 (4) instruct, coach, prepare, or assist another person to make
20 a false, misleading, or deceptive material statement concerning the
