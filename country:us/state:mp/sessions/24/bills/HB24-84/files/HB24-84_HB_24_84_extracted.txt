A BILL FOR AN ACT
To authorize the expenditure of $2,105,563.00 identified by the
Governor as a surplus from Fiscal Year 2025 revenues that shall
be appropriated by the Legislature to the Public School System
and to the 25% Retirees' Pension Obligation.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE 24TH NORTHERN MARIANAS
COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 Section 1. Findings and Purpose. The Legislature finds that the Governor,
2 in a communication dated December 31, 2025, notified the presiding officers of the
3 Fiscal Year 2025 Annual Report. The Governor reported that after accounting for
4 debt service and earmarked funds, total resources appropriated for FY 2025 under
5 Public Law 24-01 amounted to $133,516,276.00. Total General Fund expenditures
6 for the fiscal year totaled $131.4 million. Overall spending and obligations
7 remained outside appropriated limits, resulting in a surplus of $2,105,563.00.
8 The Legislature further finds that the Governor identified $2,105,563.00 in
9 surplus revenues for Fiscal Year 2025. These revenues can be immediately applied
10 toward urgent government priorities, including constitutional obligations and to

2 The Legislature additionally finds that Article XV, Section 1(e) of the
3 Commonwealth Constitution mandates that twenty-five percent (25%) of all
4 general revenues shall be appropriated to the Public School System. Consistent with
5 this constitutional requirement, $526,390.75, representing twenty-five percent
6 (25%) of the identified revenues, must be allocated to the Public School System for
7 Fiscal Year 2026 operations.
8 The Legislature further finds that after satisfying the constitutional
9 allocation to the Public School System, the remaining seventy-five percent (75%)
10 of the identified revenues—$1,579,172.25— shall be allocated into the 25%
11 Retirees' Pension Benefits ORG # 11884200 exclusively for the payment of
12 retirees' 25% pension benefits.
13 Accordingly, the purpose of this Act is to authorize the Secretary of Finance
14 to utilize $2,105,563.00 in surplus from Fiscal Year 2025 revenues as follows:
15 (a) $526,390.75 shall be allocated and expended for the Public School
16 System in compliance with Article XV, Section 1(e) of the Commonwealth
17 Constitution; and
18 (b) $1,579,172.25 shall be allocated into the 25% Retirees' Pension Benefits
19 ORG # 11884200 exclusively for the payment of retirees' 25% pension benefits.
20 Section 2. Enactment. Notwithstanding any laws, rules, or regulations to
21 the contrary, the Secretary of Finance is hereby authorized to utilize $2,105,563.00
