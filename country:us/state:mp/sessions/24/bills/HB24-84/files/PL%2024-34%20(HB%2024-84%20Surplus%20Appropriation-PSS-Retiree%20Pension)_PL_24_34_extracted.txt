Dear Mr. Speaker and Mr. President:
This is to inform you that I have signed into law House Bill No. 24-84, entitled, "To authorize the
expenditure of $2,105,563.00 identified by the Governor as a surplus from Fiscal Year 2025
revenues that shall be appropriated by the Legislature to the Public School System and to the 25%
Retirees' Pension Obligation.", which was passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate of
the Twenty-Fourth Northern Marianas Commonwealth Legislature.
This bill becomes Public Law No. 24-34. Copies bearing my signature are forwarded for your
reference.

MARIANAS COMMONWEALTH LEGISLATURE:
1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Legislature finds that the Governor, in a communication dated
3 December 31, 2025, notified the presiding officers of the Fiscal Year 2025 Annual
4 Report. The Governor reported that after accounting for debt service and
5 earmarked funds, total resources appropriated for FY 2025 under Public Law 24-
6 01 amounted to $133,516,276.00. Total General Fund expenditures for the fiscal
7 year totaled $131.4 million. Overall spending and obligations remained outside
8 appropriated limits, resulting in a surplus of $2,105,563.00.

1 The Legislature further finds that the Governor identified $2,105,563.00 in
2 surplus revenues for Fiscal Year 2025. These revenues can be immediately applied
3 toward urgent government priorities, including constitutional obligations and to
4 ensure that the 25% Retirees' Pension Benefits for this fiscal year are adequate.
5 The Legislature additionally finds that Article XV, Section 1(e) of the
6 Commonwealth Constitution mandates that twenty-five percent (25%) of all
7 general revenues shall be appropriated to the Public School System. Consistent
8 with this constitutional requirement, $526,390.75, representing twenty-five
9 percent (25%) of the identified revenues, must be allocated to the Public School
10 System for Fiscal Year 2026 operations.
11 The Legislature further finds that after satisfying the constitutional
12 allocation to the Public School System, the remaining seventy-five percent (75%)
13 of the identified revenues—$1,579,172.25—shall be allocated into the
14 25% Retirees' Pension Benefits ORG # 11884200 exclusively for the payment of
15 retirees' 25% pension benefits.
16 Accordingly, the purpose of this Act is to authorize the Secretary of Finance
17 to utilize $2,105,563.00 in surplus from Fiscal Year 2025 revenues as follows:
18 (a) $526,390.75 shall be allocated and expended for the Public School System
19 in compliance with Article XV, Section 1(e) of the Commonwealth Constitution;
20 and
21 (b) $1,579,172.25 shall be allocated into the 25% Retirees' Pension Benefits
22 ORG # 11884200 exclusively for the payment of retirees' 25% pension benefits.
23 SECTION 2. ENACTMENT.
24 Notwithstanding any laws, rules, or regulations to the contrary, the
25 Secretary of Finance is hereby authorized to utilize $2,105,563.00 in surplus from
