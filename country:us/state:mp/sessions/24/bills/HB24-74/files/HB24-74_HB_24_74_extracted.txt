1 Section 1. Findings and Purpose. The Legislature recognizes the acute
2 reliance of many residents of the Commonwealth on benefits from the federal
3 Nutrition Assistance Program (NAP) to meet their household dietary needs.
4 The Legislature finds that, due to the ongoing shutdown of the federal
5 government, the federal government will not be paying the full household
6 allotments of NAP benefits for November 2025. Pursuant to two federal court
7 orders requiring the federal government to issue benefits, the United States
8 Department of Agriculture has advised that for November 2025 the federal
9 government will pay half the full amount of household allotments.
10 As only half the full benefits will be paid by the federal government for the
11 month of November 2025, legislative action is urgently needed to compensate for
12 this shortfall. Pursuant to 1 CMC § 7604(b), the Secretary of Finance has informed
13 the Legislature that additional funds will be available for appropriation in the
