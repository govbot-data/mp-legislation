REPRESENTATIVES.

submitted Conference Committee Report 24-2.

1 SECTION 1. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
2 The Legislature acknowledges Governor David Apatang's Gov. Msg. No. 24-
3 171, which stated that the Nutrition Assistance Program (NAP) appropriation of
4 $1,000,000 is no longer needed due to the conclusion of the federal shutdown on
5 November 12, 2025, thereby averting the CNMI NAP crisis.
6 However, the Legislature finds that a critical need exists to appropriate the
7 $1,000,000 to support essential government operations. Accordingly, the
8 Legislature determines that the funds shall be allocated to the operations of the
9 Marianas Visitors Authority to augment its tourism recovery efforts, and twenty-
10 five percent (25%) of the total appropriation shall be provided to the Public School

4 regulations to the contrary, the following provision is hereby enacted into law:
5 "§ 101. Appropriation.
6 "§ 101. Appropriation.
7 $1,000,000.00 of funds derived from the preliminary distribution of
8 trust income proceeds from the Marianas Public Land Trust is hereby
9 appropriated as follows:
10 (a) $250,000.00 for the Public School System in accordance with the
11 CNMI Constitution Article XV, Section 1(e), and the funds shall be used for
12 instructional expenses;
13 (b) $750,000.00 for the operations of the Marianas Visitors Authority.
14 Section 102. Expenditure Authority.
15 (a) The Commissioner of the Public School System shall be the
16 expenditure authority of the funds under Section 101(a).
17 (b) The Marianas Visitors Authority Managing Director shall be the
18 expenditure authority of the funds provided under Section 101(b);
19 Section 103. Reporting Requirement.
20 (a) The Commissioner of the Public School System shall submit a report
21 to the presiding officers of the legislature upon the expenditure of the funds
22 provided under Section 101(a).
